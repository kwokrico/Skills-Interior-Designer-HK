## HK residential trade sequence (summary)

```text
清拆 → 水電 → 坭水/防水 → 木工（前期）→ 油漆 → 木工（後期）→ 雜項 → 交收
```

**Critical gates**

| Gate | Rule (HKEDCA) |
|---|---|
| Before demolition | Insurance; estate application (裝修/搭棚); confirm items to retain; gas hob removal by registered contractor |
| Before concealed MEP close-out | Water + power rough-in inspected |
| Before fixed joinery | Plaster (批盪) dry; concealed pipes/cables complete |
| Before timber flooring | Window glass installed (rain protection) |
| Before handover | Snag closeout; cleaning stages complete; acceptance per §交收/5–6 |
