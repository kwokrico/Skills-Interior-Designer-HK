## Limits and decisions

- These are functional HK counterparts, not claims that the US and HK instruments have identical scope or legal effect.
- No wholesale numerical table conversion was attempted. Selected values carry clause and scope; other decisions require the actual current document and specialist review.
- The accessibility publication is newer than the 2025 BFA manual, but APP-176 expressly distinguishes provisions already in regulations from advisory additions. This distinction controls the new guidance.
- The BD fire-code landing page encountered during search still described June 2023, whereas the official consolidated PDF and code index identify the 2024 edition. The PDF was used for the stair checks.
- APP-110's generic link resolves to the signed revision identifying January 2026; the appendix still bears a June 2024 date. The adaptation identifies the note's current revision without dating every diagram as new.
- WSD index verification does not claim review of every April 2026 technical requirement. Likewise, BEAM manual availability is not a credit-by-credit assessment.
- The energy FAQ contains historical examples and mixed wording at some thresholds; the addition routes threshold decisions to Schedule 3/BEC §10 instead of encoding equipment thresholds from FAQ examples.
- Composition, colour, proportion and furniture activity planning remain useful without invented local equivalents. Individual user/product checks replace unsupported assumptions about an average HK body size.
