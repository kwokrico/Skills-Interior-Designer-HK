# DECO-TV batch 4 — focused verification, 2026-11-08

Paraphrased retrieval notes, not full reproductions. These checks support the safety/evidence boundaries in the linked skill references; they do not certify any advertised product.

- [WSD inside-services controls](https://www.wsd.gov.hk/en/plumbing-engineering/enhancement-of-control-of-inside-services/index.html): supporting documents for lead-free soldering materials; non-destructive solder-joint checks and water sampling for heavy metals. [WSD water-quality programme](https://www.wsd.gov.hk/en/core-businesses/water-quality/action-plan-for-enhancing-of-drinking-water-safety/drinking-water-standards-enhanced-water-quality/index.html): prohibits leaded solder in inside services and describes permission/material/commissioning controls. Used to reject the clips' material/handwork shortcut and tissue-colour test; apply current project requirements through licensed plumber/WSD.
- [EMSD Household Electrical Safety Handbook](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_442/A01_Hshld_Elctrcl_Sfty_Hndbk.pdf): distinguishes appliance, plug/adaptor, charger, travel-adaptor and installation checks, including rated electrical input and secure components. Used for compatibility/evidence review, not a blanket approval of the demonstrated conversion plugs.
- [Fluke 1AC instruction sheet](https://media.fluke.com/bb85d503-ec0b-4a8f-a426-b10800c16021_original%20file.pdf), p.1: a non-glowing tip can coexist with live voltage; shielding, insulation, distance, user isolation, socket configuration and tester/battery condition affect indication. Used to reject no-beep proof of safe isolation; not an operating method for unqualified users.
- [HSE cutting and sanding wood](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/healthrisks/hazardous-substances/cutting-and-sanding-wood.htm): source extraction and respiratory protection form part of exposure controls. UK technical guidance, not HK legislation. [Festool dust systems](https://www.festool.com/campaigns/microsites/dustfree) also distinguishes dust classes and correct extraction. Used to reject a smell/visible-dust demonstration as grounds to dispense with respiratory protection.

No contemporary price, stock, certification, laser range, IP rating, glue model or tool specification was independently confirmed. Those remain model-documentation checks, not adopted facts. No numeric HK safety limit is inferred from overseas guidance.
