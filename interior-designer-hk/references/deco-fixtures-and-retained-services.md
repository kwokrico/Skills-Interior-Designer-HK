# Fixtures and retained-service checks

Sources: DECO-TV batch 2; [per-file coverage and archives](../../Reference/DECO-TV-BATCH2-COVERAGE-2026-11-07.md). Summary / Transcript unless caption time is specified. Product claims and incomplete visual demonstrations are not installation instructions. Chain to MEP, procurement, interface detailing and the responsible registered trades.

## Retain or replace before enclosing

Sources: `_XJasu7qlEM`, `LwlF2ul6LmE`, `XC2PXWVIvxA`, `iy1IeMTRAII`, `0R27WsvY2pU`.

- Survey developer-supplied cabinets/appliances before ordering surrounding work: actual user needs, full hardware travel, spare-part availability, filter/grease access, ventilation and whole-unit withdrawal. Compare repair/retention with replacement and consequential making good; age, price or developer identity alone is not a reason to replace everything.
- Do not tape over appliance vents or add insect mesh without manufacturer approval of the complete airflow arrangement. The source's taped oven vent is not a remedy. Resolve pest entry and cleaning with the supplier while retaining cooling and service access.
- Define whether an inspection is visual, functional, instrumented or invasive; state qualifications, limitations and permitted opening/reinstatement. No universal claim that all inspectors only check scratches or that every new flat requires destructive investigation. Damp, powdering and blistering need measured investigation; touch alone cannot identify source or establish that a past leak is resolved.
- Coordinate accessible AC condensate routes/falls and service points before joinery/ceiling closure. Use [cooling and condensation](deco-hvac-maintenance.md) for diagnosis. A short sink demonstration with no spoken steps does not establish a complete drainage test method.
- Record supply phase and verified available capacity, appliance loads, circuits and protective arrangements with the registered electrical contractor. A three-phase board label alone does not prove spare capacity; functional outlet checks remain relevant. For repeated heater tripping, stop use and obtain competent fault investigation; do not bypass switches, protection or run a direct-to-meter feed based on the transcript.

## Shower controls and screens

Sources: `R5SRXiDxSj0`, `AEIKULAoltM`.

**Safety halt:** identify the water-heater type before choosing an on/off shower head. For a shower-type (low-pressure storage) electric water heater, do not install an outlet on/off valve or shutoff shower head. Closing the main tap afterwards does not make that incompatible installation acceptable. Use the specified unrestricted outlet arrangement and refer uncertainty to the supplier/competent installer. [EMSD electric water-heater guidance](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/en/electricity_safety/publications/guidance_notes_guidelines/safety_tips_for_using_electric_water_heaters/index.html), checked 2026-11-07, identifies excessive pressure/explosion risk. See also [compliance](compliance.md).

- For other systems, verify actual heater, hose, pressure/flow, mixer and control compatibility; never generalise the source's instruction to remove a black washer. Use specified seals/connectors. A handheld switch is not thermostatic scald protection or justification for unsupervised child bathing; evaluate accessible controls and safe temperature control separately.
- For shower screens compare framed/frameless, opening type, clear entry, swing/parking, cleaning and user reach. Measure finished openings, wall plumb, steps/columns and junctions; price extra infill panels, special geometry and finishes explicitly. Confirm safety glazing, panel size/thickness, hardware/support, waterproof penetrations and sealing with the supplier. “8 mm” in one listing and “few frameless choices” are not general design rules; clear/frosted describes appearance, not safety-glass type.

## Drainage and kitchen alterations

Sources: `3F5FM55F-Wg`, `MGk3MWk0XWs`, `Ocr9CsC0wnQ`, `DxE-6A0iFgY`, `a533fimOsxI`.

- Identify the installed trap/water seal and venting arrangement; shape labels such as U-trap and bottle trap do not establish suitability for every fixture. For washing machines, coordinate the approved waste/trap arrangement with the appliance manual and plumbing design rather than add a second trap blindly. Persistent odour needs investigation of drying, siphonage, leakage, blockage and connections. Do not generalise “1:99” to unspecified cleaners or promise that one pour cures the fault.
- A WC relocation needs a route/level survey to the soil stack, suitable falls/connections, venting and maintenance access, plus permissions. “Closer to the stack” is a coordination preference, not a verified drainage design or permission to drill a slab.
- Switching from gas to electric does not authorise removal of a kitchen enclosure. Check approved plans, structural and fire-resisting functions and the applicable approval path before alteration; retain gas-contractor requirements for gas disconnection. [BD domestic alteration FAQ](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/faq/index_alteration_and_addition_works_in_domestic_premises.html), checked 2026-11-07. For facade/common-part work, review the DMC, management requirements and statutory scope separately. Do not conceal unauthorised work beneath mosaic tiles, assume management consent is statutory approval, or infer insurance coverage from a video.

## Controls and lighting within furniture

Sources: `d8W-pxmBekw`, `e1yn-rbmxcg`, `AEgmpaRGhgU`.

- A smart power strip needs model-specific total/per-outlet limits, compatible loads, local controls and tested behaviour after network/power loss. Do not infer surge protection, energy savings or safe unattended operation from Wi-Fi/voice control. Preserve continuously required equipment; a remote-off state is not safe electrical isolation. Record account handover, control labels and support requirements.
- For USB sockets verify connector/protocol, per-port and shared output, device compatibility, back-box depth and electrical suitability. Amp labels alone do not determine charging speed. Coordinate decorative frames and modules within the manufacturer's approved system; fixed electrical work remains with registered trades.
- Compare a ceiling cove with cabinet-top/back/undershelf lighting as design alternatives. Draw the light path, glare/sightlines, task coverage, power/driver access, heat management and replaceability before cabinet manufacture. Decorative indirect light does not by itself meet task illuminance. See the lighting module and material schedule.

## Decorative USB faceplates — batch 3

Sources: 6xDoSByokzA, ayJWJcGimQU, t-j412mdh6k; [coverage and archives](../../Reference/DECO-TV-BATCH3-COVERAGE-2026-11-07.md).

Coordinate switch/socket colours and physical samples, including fingerprints and approved cleaning of coated metal finishes. Before replacing a mains outlet with USB-only charging, confirm remaining mains-socket needs, actual back-box space, module compatibility and maintenance/replacement access. Distinguish shared USB output from per-port output and verify the device/protocol combination; an old 2.1/3.1/4.2 A claim or one meter demonstration is not a universal charging-speed specification. Require model-matched safety documents and current warranty terms; plastic behind metal, insurance, “all certificates”, automatic charge cutoff or alleged EMSD endorsement in an advert does not itself prove safe installation or remove earthing/protection checks by the responsible registered electrical trade. No DIY wiring instructions are inferred.

## Adaptors, electrical testing and accessory claims — batch 4

Sources: [batch 4 coverage](../../Reference/DECO-TV-BATCH4-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md). Apply the existing registered-electrical-trade boundary.

- Imported appliances/adaptors (2UFcFwCFscg, xqh8R1pn-iE): physical fit is only one check. Verify appliance input voltage/frequency, power/current, earthing/class, compatible plug/conversion device and relevant safety evidence. A mechanical adaptor does not establish voltage conversion or make every two-pin product suitable for Hong Kong. Do not force a bulky plug into an enclosure, defeat shutters, or generalise the source's “all foreign appliances become legal” claim. Use [EMSD household safety guidance](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_442/A01_Hshld_Elctrcl_Sfty_Hndbk.pdf), checked 2026-11-08, and product-specific instructions rather than retailer/price rankings.
- Detector limits (X5jsANOLfrw): non-contact indication is not proof of safe isolation, absence of concealed services, insulation integrity or a broken conductor. A multifunction wall detector is not automatically equivalent to a particular voltage tester. Do not proceed to drilling or touching because nothing beeps; arrange competent identification/isolation and appropriate verification. [Fluke instruction sheet](https://media.fluke.com/bb85d503-ec0b-4a8f-a426-b10800c16021_original%20file.pdf), checked 2026-11-08, explicitly warns that voltage can remain without an indication and describes shielding/distance/battery limitations.
- Connections and earthing (gBtIDuoCB30, 1xPKFktEArg): terminal blocks in ceiling voids need the appropriate connector/enclosure, conductor capacity, securing/protection and inspection access as determined by the registered trade. Tape/heat-shrink alone is not a complete compliant connection specification. A second earth terminal is not permission for an owner to bond an aluminium window; determine the actual protective arrangement professionally. Stainless steel, nationality and extra terminals do not prove corrosion immunity or built-in circuit protection.
- Unsafe USB adaptor demonstrations (nzBxyejYGRY): stop using suspect/damaged accessories and obtain competent assessment/replacement. Do not reproduce live exposed-board probing. “5 V USB” does not establish safe mains isolation; unverified meter readings and the speaker's induction explanation are not a certified fault diagnosis or touch-current assessment.
- Extension reels and surge strips (rUwOchhtnQc, ujujtphjxIY): verify total load, cable/plug condition, coiled/uncoiled ratings and unwind instructions, placement and protective features. Do not add outlet ratings together as available supply capacity. Investigate/remove overload or fault before reset; repeated reset is not a remedy. “10,000 A surge” is not a continuous load rating or a lightning-immunity guarantee: require the named protection specification, test basis and replacement/status instructions.
- USB/smart accessory refinements (1Pe0BYdcBJo, g0fQhs1BYzg, iYUxUmnkiX8, tNoRsWw-qDc, qmOsSAPXSuw): existing model/certification/shared-output checks apply. Confirm whether each switch controls USB, mains or both; record power-loss behaviour and manual override. Check an attached phone shelf's retention, load and reachable placement, without straining charging leads or obstructing socket use. Do not translate “even on the ceiling” into an installation recommendation. Historical prices/offers and ambiguous “EM60950” transcription do not establish current certification, availability, savings or charging performance.
