# Tim Ho: procurement and separate-trade coordination

Use with tendering QA, site supervision and handover. [Coverage audit and archived extracts](../../Reference/TIM-HO-BATCH1-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md) supplies zero-based source indices. Read alongside [contractor scope and disputes](deco-contractor-scope-and-disputes.md); source anecdotes do not establish liability or contract entitlement.

## A drawing and a responsibility for each interface

Sources 5, 9, 20, 32, 39, 44, 49, 60, 70, 72, 77, 82, 90, 94, 104, 105, 109.

Agree who owns site measurement and coordinated working drawings, setting-out approval (彈墨), subsequent changes and distribution. A render or paid 360-degree model is not a coordinated construction drawing: define deliverable format, revisions, export/access rights and handover in the appointment. Identify the actual responsible designer/site contact and substitutions, rather than judging competence by founder/salesperson status or social-media following.

For split contracts, put these interfaces into the scope matrix with an owner, prerequisite, hold point and acceptance record:

| Interface | Resolve before work |
|---|---|
| Gas/demolition | Registered isolation/capping; agreed routes and responsibility for permitted chases; no unapproved structural cutting. |
| Cabinet/plumbing | Finished measurement, connection locations, cabinet installation, plumber's return visit, test and access. |
| Hood/TV/stone | Model templates, backing and brackets, fixing/penetration responsibility, finish protection and warranty. |
| Joinery/paint/sealant | Final touch-up and compatible joint sealing after installation, protection removal/reinstatement and inspection. |
| Waste/cleaning | Packaging and trade waste, internal cabinet/appliance cleaning, scope boundaries, inspection and remaining snag work. Cleaning is not completion or IAQ clearance. |
| Security/handover | Retain safe security during replacement; control temporary access keys/codes and revoke on handover. Do not use common fire equipment as storage for keys. |

Mark uninstalled fixtures, leaks and sharp/unsafe handles as outstanding work, not completed merely because a final walk-through occurs. Record concealed routes before closing and assess actual service positions before late toilet/TV/cabinet drilling.

## Comparable prices and decisions

For package cabinetry, state measurement axes, height/depth, disconnected runs, hardware, delivery stages and omission credits. Separate survey fees from construction price. For TBC/provisional/optional items, show quantity basis, rate or allowance, exclusions, decision date and effect on total; an unknown is not zero. Independently reconcile quantity × rate, subtotals, allowances and the issued version before signing.

Confirm variations through the authorised contract process with price/time effects and decision deadline. Preserve chat records and recap oral instructions, but a read receipt is not acceptance and a chat is not an automatic legal conclusion. Do not promise that cancellation/refund cures an estimating error or that delay deductions are unlimited. Tender scoring should use stated, evidenced criteria and realistic programme assumptions; interview headcount or apparent sincerity is not competence evidence. A professional signature does not legitimise borrowed registrations or unqualified execution.

Imported goods need samples, installation manuals, compatible local connections, delivery/assembly scope, returns, spares and downtime planning. Product nationality, promotional prices and historical supplier refund policies do not establish quality or current entitlement.

## Insurance is separate from the defects obligation

Sources 19 and 102. Check named insureds, actual employer/principal/subcontractor arrangements, works and locations (including exterior windows, scaffolds and AC), policy dates, exclusions, limits and deductibles. Distinguish employees' compensation, public liability and contract-works/property cover; one certificate is not all three. A contractual DLP is not the same as an insurance maintenance-period extension. Arrange needed extensions before expiry; do not adopt a universal 30-day minimum or assert that all later extensions are impossible. Agree who bears a deductible under the actual contract, rather than requiring automatic advance payment.

The source's HK$20 million employees' compensation figure is not a current minimum. The [Labour Department concise guide](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/public/ecd/pco360.pdf), checked 2026-11-08, states minimum cover per event of HK$100 million for not more than 200 employees and HK$200 million for more than 200; its principal-contractor construction-project policy provision specifies at least HK$200 million. Verify the applicable arrangement and current requirements with the insurer/adviser. Do not infer automatic homeowner/owners' corporation liability, or that an annual policy is inherently invalid, from the videos.

## Follow-up batch: pre-work and service scope

[Follow-up audit and 13 archived extracts](../../Reference/TIM-HO-BATCH2-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md), indices 0, 2, 4, 6, 8–10: identify the actual contracting party, subcontracting layers, supervisor capacity, drawing responsibility and payment/variation authority. Do not assume five sites is a universal supervisor limit, commercial trades always have inferior workmanship, or discounts/deductions described in anecdotes are contractually valid.

State consultation/survey fees, any credit on appointment, deliverables, revision limits and design reuse rights before engagement; paid design is not inherently dishonest and free consultation is not a universal entitlement. Specify owner-supplied goods advice, receipt/inspection authority, delivery attendance, photographs of damage, notification deadlines and storage risk. Distinguish an agreed receipt inspection from unconditional acceptance. Aftercare response/call-out fees and exclusions must be written, including after DLP expiry.

Before demolition, mark and inventory retained items **inside** cabinets/voids as well as exposed items; brief the demolition team and control removal/disposal. Remove personal valuables and agree custody of retained goods. Inspect retained items immediately after demolition and before waste removal, so mistaken removal can be identified promptly. For partial occupied works, settle scope and dependable delivery dates before demolition where feasible, allowing for final site measurement and unforeseen conditions; premanufacture must not outrun verified dimensions.
