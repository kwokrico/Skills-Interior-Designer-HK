# Hong Kong application of Ching's interior-design topics

**Official sources checked: 7 November 2026.** Load when applying Ching, ADA/IBC/NFPA examples, US building-service conventions or historical LEED credits to an HK project. This reference supplements [spatial composition](ching-spatial-composition.md) and [furnishings](ching-furnishings-and-window-treatments.md). It supplies local decision rules; it does not certify a design.

## Choose the local basis before using a dimension

Keep the book's transferable methods: activity planning, mass/void, proportion, visual hierarchy, colour relationships and furniture grouping. Localise the constraints around them. Do not convert an inch dimension to millimetres and label it an HK requirement, or invent a single “Hong Kong body size.” Test the actual users, mobility aids, activities and selected products.

For each project record use/occupancy, existing approved arrangements, proposed changes, responsible professional, governing document/edition, applicable clause, and evidence still required. Separate **statutory requirement**, **code/practice-note compliance guidance**, **advisory design target**, **certification criterion**, and **contract/product requirement**. A newer publication or a stricter-looking overseas number does not by itself determine local applicability. Apply [compliance](compliance.md) and obtain the AP/RSE or relevant specialist's review for regulated decisions.

## 1. Access, anthropometrics and inclusive interiors

**Legal-status gate:** BD's September 2026 [APP-176, paragraphs 3–7](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP176.pdf) directs AP/RSEs to B(P)R regulations 72–73 and the Third/Fourth Schedules for statutory requirements. DMUA provisions outside those existing regulations/schedules are presently advisory, including provisions labelled obligatory but proposed for future legislation. Do not call the whole manual mandatory or discard existing statutory obligations. Existing-building constraints do not create an automatic exemption: any exemption/modification is for the Building Authority to consider on the case's merits.

Use the [Design Manual on Universal Accessibility 2026](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/DMUA2026e.pdf) as the local design resource:

- Chapters 3–7: applicability, arrival, horizontal/vertical circulation and lifts.
- Chapter 9: sanitary provision; coordinate the complete transfer, fixture and door arrangement.
- Chapter 10: handrails, lighting, counters, signs, controls and sensory access.
- Chapter 11: elderly-friendly and dementia design, maintenance and constrained existing buildings.
- Appendices A–D: anthropometrics, wheelchair movement, flooring slip resistance, luminance/colour contrast; Appendix E identifies provisions proposed for legislation.

For example, §5.3(1) gives **1200 mm clear width for an accessible route**; it is not a universal minimum for every aisle inside every home. Apply Chapter 3 scope and APP-176 status first, then check door approach, manoeuvring, protrusions and other applicable requirements. A turning circle alone is not a complete accessibility review.

Designer application: place the required clear/transfer envelopes on the furnishing plan and check them with doors, drawers and movable furniture in use. Review sensory legibility alongside visual styling; use the relevant local contrast/slip method rather than substituting a colour name or generic “anti-slip” claim.

## 2. Fire, stairs, barriers and finishes

Replace US exit rules with the [BD Fire Safety Code 2011, 2024 Edition](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/fs_code2011.pdf). Use Part B for escape, C for fire-resisting construction and E for component evidence. Exit capacity requires the applicable classification, occupancy and route configuration; a width-only calculator is not HK approval.

**Required staircases, B14.2–B14.4 (printed p.44; PDF p.53):** straight flights without winders, generally 2–16 risers; general tread minimum 225 mm clear of nosings and riser maximum 175 mm. **Exceptions:** use 5a requires tread ≥280 mm/riser ≤150 mm; use 5b requires tread ≥250 mm/riser 75–150 mm. Landings must meet B14.3; headroom ≥2000 mm and width per B8.1. For **5a**, also apply Part B Section 3: **B23.1(b)–(d)** supplies additional flight step-count and turn requirements; do not apply the general flight rule alone. There must not be more than two successive flights without a turn, and a turn is needed between successive flights if either exceeds 12 steps. Check B23.2–B23.3 for returning handrails and guarded recesses (printed p.55; PDF p.64). Coordinate applicable accessibility provisions before detailing; these checks do not certify approval.

Separately check the [FSD installation/maintenance codes](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/fire_protection/notices/code.html), whose index identifies September 2022 as the current edition and retains 1994 provisions for specified upgrading regimes. Confirm the applicable regime and subsequent circulars with the fire-service specialist. Do not assume a US sprinkler detail or a generic “fire-rated” product label meets the project's requirements.

**Protective barriers:** [APP-110, January 2026 revision, paragraphs 2–5](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/signed/APP110se.pdf) gives height ≥1.1 m, a solid lowest 150 mm subject to its enclosed-stair exception, and openings that inhibit passage of objects over 100 mm in their smallest dimension. Measure from the adjoining surface people can step on; apply the paragraph 3 curb conditions rather than measuring from a convenient lower slab. Check loads, climbability and the complete connection. A handrail's height does not establish barrier compliance. Do not authorise glass/anchor replacement from these geometric checks; existing AP/RSE hard stops remain.

Designer application: specify fire performance for the relevant wall/ceiling/floor/door assembly and its interfaces, with test evidence accepted under the applicable HK requirement. Do not translate an ASTM/UL designation into a claimed local rating without verifying acceptance and assembly scope.

## 3. Windows, daylight and room subdivision

[APP-130, paragraphs 1–3 and Appendices A–C](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP130.pdf) provides an alternative performance-based route for natural lighting/ventilation in domestic habitable rooms and kitchens under B(P)R regulations 30–32. It involves justified assessment and modification of prescriptive requirements, not an owner's automatic choice to remove a window. An AP must substantiate alternative assessment methods.

Designer application: before moving partitions or adding tall joinery/window coverings, map which opening serves each room, available light/openable area, obstruction, and the proposed room use. Confirm the applicable approval route before replacing natural provision with electric light or air conditioning. A bright rendering, borrowed light through a partition or a split air conditioner is not compliance evidence by itself.

## 4. Electrical, water supply and drainage

**Electrical edition:** the [EMSD wiring-code index](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/en/electricity_safety/publications/codes_of_practice/index.html) lists the 2025 edition, published 31 December 2025. Its grace provision also accepts installations/parts complying with the 2020 edition **if completed and connected to supply by 31 December 2026**. Record completion/connection dates and confirm the applicable edition; contract award date alone does not meet that condition. Recheck this transition for later projects.

**Electrical work:** [EMSD's installation guidance](https://eld.emsd.gov.hk/eic/en/safety-messages/safetyb01.html) requires a registered electrical contractor, inspection/testing by a registered electrical worker before energisation, and a WR1 completion certificate. The owner retains WR1; do not confuse it with periodic-test WR2 endorsement. Designer application: schedule actual appliance loads, outlet/control positions and access, then obtain circuit/protection design from the electrical specialist. Do not copy US outlet, circuit or bathroom-zone details.

**Water supply:** [WSD's technical index](https://www.wsd.gov.hk/en/plumbing-engineering/requirements-for-plumbing-installation/technical-requirements-for-plumbing-works-in-bldgs/index.html) lists April 2026 requirements, figures and amendment table. Check the applicable circular/transition and accepted fittings with the licensed plumber. WSD's [works-of-a-minor-nature guidance](https://www.wsd.gov.hk/en/plumbing-engineering/works-of-a-minor-nature/index.html) distinguishes permission-waived minor works from other works needing prior application through a licensed plumber and execution by designated persons. A permission waiver does not waive workmanship/material requirements.

**Drainage is a separate check:** [BD's kitchen/bathroom alteration guidance](https://www.careyourbuilding.bd.gov.hk/en/building_safety_tips/34.html) distinguishes replacement of non-embedded internal branches/fitments to the original approved design from alterations, including relocation with additional drainage. The latter can engage minor-works items 2.30/3.23; confirm every item condition and qualified contractor rather than self-classifying. Designer application: reserve falls, traps/venting and maintenance access; identify new penetrations and shared connections. WSD supply acceptance is not drainage approval.

## 5. Lighting/HVAC energy, IAQ and sustainability

**Energy:** [EMSD's code index](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_beeo_codtechguidelines.html) lists BEC 2024 and its technical guidance; use the linked effective-date circular. Screen building type, installation scope, phased works and completion dates with a Registered Energy Assessor before fixing lighting/HVAC specifications. [EMSD's major-retrofitting FAQ](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html) explains the 500 m² area-related route, aggregation within 12 months and installation-specific conditions, and identifies two main types of major retrofitting works. **500 m² is not a universal exemption test**: review Schedule 3 and BEC §10/Table 10.1, including central-service works. Finishes-only work does not automatically trigger the same duties as services replacement. Do not reduce compliance to wattage or lux alone.

**IAQ:** the [April 2026 IAQ certification guide, §§2–7 and Annexes 2–4](https://www.iaq.gov.hk/common/form/Guide_on_IAQ_Certification_Scheme_eng.pdf) addresses a voluntary scheme for eligible offices/public places served by MVAC, with Good/Excellent classes. Split/window air conditioners alone do not fall within that MVAC definition; qualifying mixed systems may. Do not promise certification for an ordinary home using only split units. Plan walkthrough, source/moisture control, representative measurements and mould assessment with the accredited certificate issuing body. Low-VOC paint or a single handheld reading is not a certificate or proof of whole-room air quality. Use the guide's current methods/objectives rather than treating its limits as universal residential law.

**Local green fit-out assessment:** [HKGBC's manual register](https://www.hkgbc.org.hk/eng/beam-plus/beam-plus-references/manuals-assessment/manuals-assessment.jsp) lists BEAM Plus Interiors v2.0, separate residential and non-residential manuals (2023 editions), available for registration at review date. Select the applicable tool, current circulars and registered version; map each proposed credit to its actual evidence and responsible party. The book's LEED 2008/2009 points do not convert into BEAM points. Certification criteria remain distinct from statutory approvals; a client may also contractually require LEED or another scheme.

## 6. Acoustics and construction controls

Keep room-acoustic and sound-isolation concepts; specify the required test method, assembly and project performance instead of inventing an HK-wide STC/NRC minimum. Environmental noise compliance is separate from internal acoustic comfort. For works, check the [EPD construction-noise permit route](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/application_for_licences/guidance/application_maincontent36.html) for powered mechanical equipment/prescribed construction work, plus estate hours. Neither estate permission nor a satisfactory room-acoustic calculation grants a noise permit.
