# Small repairs and trade-material decisions

Source: DECO-TV 家居DIY 要搵裝修WHY, 泥水俱樂部, 點止撈哥咁簡單 and selected 裝修速成功; [inventory](../../Reference/DECO-TV-COLLECTION-2026-11-07.md). Audio summaries are not complete visual method statements. Keep demonstration claims separate from approved products and competent installation.

- Plumbing symptoms: record slow drainage, changing WC water level, odour and gurgling; investigate traps, venting, blockages and connections rather than equating every odour with a broken pipe. Request identifiable diagnostic evidence before invasive repair. Do not prescribe pressure blasts, powered snakes or mixed drain chemicals from entertainment demonstrations.
- Burst supply: avoid wet electrical areas, stop use, identify a safely accessible local isolation valve; if the failure is upstream of it or isolation is uncertain, contact management/emergency plumbing service for the appropriate supply isolation. Temporary tape/putty does not constitute pressure-pipe repair. Confirm freshwater versus flushing-water systems and affected users.
- WC replacement parts: identify manufacturer/model, inlet/flush-valve assembly, seals, fixing dimensions, supply and service access. 波曲 commonly refers to the inlet/float valve; 缸芯/水芯 to flush components; verify the actual part. Matching appearance or one diameter is insufficient. A new valve insert fits an existing base only where the maker confirms it.
- Furniture repair: assess substrate integrity and supported load before hinge-hole filling, edge-band replacement or reinforcement. Loose/crumbled board may require panel/component replacement. Use a compatible adhesive/repair system with its cure and load limits; toothpicks or unidentified putty do not prove durable hinge anchorage. Heat-activated edging needs the actual edging/adhesive temperature limits and protected adjacent finishes.
- Door locks: check backset, bore, door thickness, handing, spindle/latch/strike and required egress/fire performance before changing knob/lever/electronic hardware. Incomplete audio cannot supply missing steps. Broken-key extraction can damage the cylinder; refer difficult cases to a locksmith rather than instruct glue/hammer improvisation.
- Epoxy/steel putty, remouldable plastics and “wet-surface” sealants need actual substrate, temperature, pressure, potable-water, fire and structural suitability evidence. Short basin impact/leak demonstrations and noodle repairs do not establish long-term sanitary or load-bearing reliability. Isolate unsafe cracked sanitaryware and obtain a suitable repair/replacement assessment.
- Timber ordering: explicitly identify imperial units versus other trade measures. In the source's imperial convention, 1 foot = 304.8 mm, 1 inch = 25.4 mm and 1 分 = 1/8 inch = 3.175 mm; thus nominal 6分 is 19.05 mm and 4×8 feet is 1219.2×2438.4 mm. These are conversions, not guaranteed supplied board dimensions. Confirm actual thickness, sheet size, grain, finish faces, cut tolerances, kerf and special-shape capability on the cut list.
- Route purchases to the relevant specialist: plumbing fittings, sanitaryware, electrical supplies, timber cutting or paint tinting. Give model/product/colour codes and samples; historic shop promotions, deposits and prices are not current offers.
- Masonry: compare actual block/brick product, wall load, thickness, moisture, fire/acoustic evidence and fixing design. Red-brick versus cement-brick generalizations cannot determine anchor capacity or carbon superiority. Specify render/bed/adhesive from substrate, tile and system requirements; a site 1:3 mix or “hard bed/wet bed” discussion is not a universal specification. Coordinate flatness, falls, cure and finish build-up before laying.
- Painting: choose brush/roller material and nap for coating, substrate and intended texture; trial for shedding and appearance. Price or one bad wool-roller demonstration does not establish a material-family ranking. Follow product thinning, ventilation, working time and recoat limits; avoid reworking solvent coatings as they tack. Do not adopt a speaker's dilution or wait time universally.
- Excluded as instructions: moving while standing on a ladder, improvised wrench extensions/blade snapping, unclear wire stripping/splicing/taping and capacitor work, incomplete assembly audio and unverified glove ratings. Select task-appropriate tools/PPE from actual specifications and safe working methods. Airline lithium-battery/tool policies need current airline/airport checks for the actual journey; archived allowances are not reusable current rules.
- Appliance usefulness: assess preparation, cleaning, drying, storage, service access and time saved, not bundled accessory count alone. Standby reduction must preserve equipment needing continuous service; no blanket shutdown rule for water systems, refrigeration or safety equipment.

## Tool selection and measurement — DECO-TV batch 4

Sources and limitations: [44-file review](../../Reference/DECO-TV-BATCH4-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md). These are selection/inspection checks, not substitute operating instructions.

- Laser distance/level instruments (6BHH5Dwi1Sg, vPRch_n5fCE): record model, units, front/rear measuring reference, target surface, datum and calibration/function check. Cross-check critical fabrication dimensions independently; keep sketches with measurements rather than relying on two stored readings. Confirm range/accuracy and the complete IP rating from actual documentation: the transcript's “100 mm”, inferred “100 m” and incomplete “IP5” are not verified specifications. A silent levelling alarm does not prove calibration; confirm permitted setup and laser precautions from the manual.
- Cutting/sanding (0208bIafWrE, AEhWkA_ddQc, hnRIoEyrUPg): match blade/tooth geometry, speed, depth, guide rail, support and extraction to material/tool instructions and an approved trial cut. More teeth do not establish twice the cutting speed; country of manufacture and a clean demo cut do not certify quality. Align abrasive extraction holes with the pad and maintain the compatible extractor/filter system. Do not accept “no smell/no visible dust, therefore no respirator”. Assess exposure and required respiratory protection alongside extraction. [HSE wood-cutting/sanding guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/healthrisks/hazardous-substances/cutting-and-sanding-wood.htm), checked 2026-11-08, supports both controls; it is UK technical guidance, not HK legislation.
- Hearing protection (34n1B8yX77I): select rated protection for the assessed task/noise, fit and compatibility with other PPE. Comfort alone is not attenuation evidence; entertainment earbuds under earmuffs are not a verified hearing-protection system, and earmuffs do not replace required impact/eye protection.
- Drilling and hand tools (IuZ6Xc-WQ7g, L92Wliu2nHQ, VpJPbfpHZ5s, SNg5DQgfS_w, Nxdemx-DLMg, jI50uHsoWKk, xKnVMTePa6A): choose compatible bit/shank/chuck and actual mode; trigger speed is not calibrated torque control. Centre punches can mark suitable workpieces, but require substrate/finish compatibility and trial force; do not punch brittle tile/glass or treat a mark as clearance to drill concealed services. For nail/screw extraction or filing, assess accessible grip, supported workpiece and adjacent finish protection; stop if removal damages the substrate or a critical fixing. Generic pliers are not automatically rated cutters, insulated tools or hot-work tongs. Tool ownership is not evidence of contractor competence.
- Adhesives (8U7-xOYTMUQ, O2q1CvDSqqc, g3ixMl_OE_o): select the actual substrate, joint gap, load, cure and finish requirements, with TDS/SDS. UV resin needs the specified light source, exposure and accessible cure geometry; do not infer that shadowed material has cured or prescribe a universal seconds/minutes recipe. For hot-melt guns distinguish watts, temperature, warm-up and output rate; confirm stick/nozzle compatibility and safe nozzle-change method. Do not adopt the garbled brand or “60/200 fire” units. For cyanoacrylates, verify grade numbers, low-bloom/clarity and material suitability, PPE and storage; do not universally freeze opened adhesive in a domestic food freezer or use any glove without SDS compatibility.
- Portable lighting (BGhqkdwukTU, GzMyrarMKRU): plan task coverage, glare, stable mounting, runtime at the selected setting, charging and backup supply. Claimed lumens/runtime/IP rating require model evidence; lamp ingress protection does not automatically cover an exposed charger/connector or authorise outdoor storm work. A torch can support a household outage plan but is not proof of compliant emergency escape lighting. Do not adopt candles/gas lamps as the worksite backup from this anecdote.
- Survival gadgets and the 2017 competition (THOezTWDDSM, hQwkbnJChJo, PpDACJiOuCI): out of interior technical scope except tool-planning awareness. No inferred glass-breaking/escape procedure, rescue rope capacity or fall-arrest approval from novelty products; use the project's competent emergency/access plan.

## Potable-water solder evidence — safety gate

Sources: M3v4uARmwzY and nf0-B836eX0. **Do not specify leaded solder for potable-water pipe joints**, even if easier to melt or allegedly kept out of contact with water by insertion depth. Do not use colour, shine or a tissue-rub demonstration to certify lead absence. Require traceable compliant pipe/fitting/solder documentation (including integral solder rings), the responsible licensed plumber's installation/approval route and applicable inspection, flushing and water-quality testing. Do not give a torch sequence from this novice demonstration or treat it as an approved joint detail. Suspected non-compliant joints require investigation and remediation, not cosmetic covering.

[WSD inside-services controls](https://www.wsd.gov.hk/en/plumbing-engineering/enhancement-of-control-of-inside-services/index.html) and [WSD water-quality programme](https://www.wsd.gov.hk/en/core-businesses/water-quality/action-plan-for-enhancing-of-drinking-water-safety/drinking-water-standards-enhanced-water-quality/index.html), checked 2026-11-08, establish lead-free material documentation, prohibition of leaded solder and inspection/testing controls. Confirm current requirements for the actual works; historical US dates/prices and the clip's chemical explanation are not adopted.
