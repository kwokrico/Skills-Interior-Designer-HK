# Tim Ho: finish evidence and everyday use

Use with procurement, millwork, interface detailing, ergonomics and sustainability. [Coverage audit and archived sources](../../Reference/TIM-HO-BATCH1-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md) record zero-based source indices. These are qualified field prompts, not tested product specifications.

## Specify the assembly, not the marketing name

Sources 6, 15, 26, 29, 30, 34, 36, 45, 47, 61, 62, 66, 73, 84, 92, 97, 107.

- **Microcement/art paint/diatom finishes:** obtain the named system's substrate acceptance, preparation, permitted layers, movement treatment, sealer, cure, cleaning and repair requirements. Wet-use/slip suitability and waterproofing are separate evidence questions. A sound-tile overlay is conditional, not permission to hide hollow tiles, damp or failed waterproofing. Levelling compound is not automatically a wearing finish. 'Mineral', 'acrylic', 'not detected' or a water demonstration does not establish zero emissions or pool suitability.
- **Filler and paint:** distinguish skim filler, plasterboard joint compound and exterior products; check permitted thickness, mix, drying and compatibility. Do not prescribe improvised varnish/glue primers, heat-gun drying or a universal coat count. Resolve junctions and remove sanding dust before compatible coatings.
- **炭晶板 / eco-board / wood-look metal:** obtain actual core, facing, adhesive, edge and fixing details with relevant fire/emissions evidence for the installed assembly. Product names do not prove carbon content, zero formaldehyde, non-warping or fire safety. Source 30 is too corrupted to reconstruct a technical specification.
- **SPC:** distinguish core performance from joints, underlay, substrate and perimeter details. Require manufacturer-specific moisture/flatness/thermal and expansion requirements; neither 'all SPC swells' nor 'all SPC needs no expansion' is justified. Do not rigidly seal a required movement gap or call the complete floor waterproof because a sample survives water.
- **Enamel and slabs:** assess actual substrate, edges, cutouts, supports and care. Trial magnetic accessories under intended loads; inspect cut-edge protection and reflections on the installed panel. Price or country of origin is not corrosion/impact evidence. Coin/hammer demonstrations are not abrasion or structural tests.
- **Aluminium recolouring:** agree external appearance/estate permissions, preparation, coating compatibility and protection of seals, drainage openings and adjacent finishes. Cosmetic repainting is not structural window repair or statutory inspection.

Use [finish selection](../subskills/interior-material-procurement/references/finish-selection-field-notes.md) for tile setting-out and wet-slip evidence, and [DECO finish evidence](deco-finish-evidence-and-maintenance.md) for overlays and maintenance. Show visible cuts at entrances, corridors and countertops; price tiled height and concealed areas explicitly. No video-derived grout width or R-rating alone proves shower suitability.

## Protect retained finishes through each trade

Sources 11, 33, 57, 80, 82, 85. Survey what is sound and retainable before choosing partial replacement; age or budget alone does not decide wholesale stripping. Record existing damage and responsibility for removal/reinstatement of protection. Wet MDF can transfer colour to stone: use compatible non-staining protection over a clean surface, respecting curing and moisture-release requirements. Test tape adhesion/staining and dwell time; 'masking tape' is not universally safe. Protect retained sills and windows. Do not accept concealed damp or hollow finishes merely because they are near pipes.

Storm leakage needs path diagnosis and safe qualified inspection. Temporary internal containment is not proof of permanent repair; do not block drainage or instruct exterior climbing. Ownership/liability depends on the building documents and cause, not a blanket assertion that window-edge leaks are always the occupier's responsibility.

## Fit to the person, installation and maintenance path

Sources 10, 43, 58, 64, 65, 69, 70, 76, 86, 89, 91, 98, 108, 109.

| Item | Additional review |
|---|---|
| Helper/guest room | Provide suitable accommodation and reasonable privacy; assess ventilation, safe access, usable headroom and storage with the same care as other occupants. A fan does not itself prove legal ventilation. Do not justify unsafe sleeping spaces through superstition. [LD helper guidance](https://www.fdh.labour.gov.hk/en/faq.html). |
| Platform/lift bed | Trial total platform+mattress height, trip edges, access to drawers/lids, lifting effort, pinch/entrapment risks, load-rated retention and emergency/manual operation. Keep service access. No universal platform height or lift angle; no gender-based usability assumptions. |
| Future guest/child use | Test actual mobility and guest frequency before lofts or convertible furniture. Plan sockets/backing and circulation for future layouts; age alone does not establish loft-bed safety. |
| Sliding wardrobe | Confirm actual panel dimensions/mass, hardware rating, anti-derail measures, level, stops, soft-close and adjustment access. Honeycomb construction does not guarantee no warping. |
| TV and service cabinetry | Coordinate bracket, structural backing, fixing loads, stone/finish penetrations and removable service panels. Verify cable connectors, bends and draw access. No universal backing thickness or assumption that veneer supports loads. |
| Delivery and assembly | Check delivery route and assembly working space, not just final furniture dimensions; distinguish mattress from frame size. Confirm upstairs delivery, assembly, packaging disposal, claims window and written warranty. Dispatch date is not arrival date. |
| Curtains | Measure clear opening, track, fabric fullness, returns/overlaps, stack-back, finished height and conflicts with handles/AC/skirtings. Motor power/service and child-safe cords need design. No universal fullness factor. |
| Bathroom | Trial bath/walk-in access, storage and mirror task light; plan future grab-bar backing where appropriate. A shower seat is a usability choice, not a prediction of ill health. Floating vanities need verified support and accessible services. |

## Emissions and cultural preferences

Source 107: prioritise low-emission materials and ventilation. Odour, family irritation, elapsed months or a treatment warranty do not establish acceptable IAQ; recirculating cooling alone does not supply fresh air. Treatment cannot replace source control and appropriate measurement. [HK IAQ pollutant guidance](https://www.iaq.gov.hk/en/what_is_iaq/common_indoor_air_pollutants.html) and [IAQ FAQ](https://www.iaq.gov.hk/en/faq.html).

Feng shui arrangements and auspicious dates may be recorded as client preferences, without asserting causal effects on health, fortune, safety or relationships. They cannot override escape, electrical separation, structural integrity, inclusive accommodation or approved building arrangements. Promotional prices, named-firm allegations and supplier refund promises are not general professional knowledge.
