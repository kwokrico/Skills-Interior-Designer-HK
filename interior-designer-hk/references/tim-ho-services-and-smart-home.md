# Tim Ho: service interfaces and smart-home commissioning

Use with MEP, millwork, procurement and handover modules. Supplied extracts are field examples, not product certification or installation instructions. [Per-source review and archived extracts](../../Reference/TIM-HO-BATCH1-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md) identify evidence, exclusions and transcript limitations; source numbers below are zero-based manifest indices.

## Switches, hubs, locks and cameras

Sources 14, 16, 24, 42, 51, 63, 78, 96. Before ordering, record exact model/protocol, hub dependence, local operation during internet/power loss, load compatibility, neutral requirement, backbox depth and conductor space. Separate a virtual scene button from a wired relay. Do not delete existing two-way control merely because an app can imitate it. Curtain motors need coordinated mounting, power and replacement access before ceilings close.

Fixed wiring is for registered electrical trades. Do not identify a conductor from a transcript or colour alone: old and new colours coexist; green/yellow is protective earth, not neutral. Require identification, isolation and testing. See [EMSD wiring guidance](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/m/en/electricity_safety/information_for_the_electrical_trade/electricity_news/installation_guidelines/).

Survey lock thickness, mortise, door/frame condition, gate/handrail clearance and both swings using a dimensioned photo and actual product template. Preserve required fire-door certification and escape operation; child-lock features cannot obstruct required egress. Trial operation with actual users: lift-to-lock versus automatic locking, bolt engagement, low battery, loss of power/network and emergency inside/outside access. Biometric marketing is not proof against failure or impersonation. Record owner/admin ownership, physical keys, recovery method and expiry/revocation of installer/guest credentials. Away scenes must not indiscriminately isolate critical loads or unlock gates on motion.

Mock up doorbell camera view, chime audibility, power and connectivity. Minimise capture of neighbours and shared corridors; agree mounting permissions, access, retention and deletion. Do not treat a camera as a replacement for physical security. Interior cameras must respect private rest/bathroom areas and helper privacy; use [PCPD CCTV guidance](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/resources_centre/publications/files/guidance_cctv_surveillance.pdf), not a claim that security overrides privacy law. Presence sensors are not certified fall or medical monitors merely because a demonstration says so.

## Appliance fit includes servicing

Sources 0, 2, 4, 12, 21, 27, 35, 56, 60, 74, 93, 100, 103, 106, 109.

| Interface | Evidence before release |
|---|---|
| Multi-split AC | Combined capacity and simultaneous demand; outdoor access; shared-unit outage consequence; model-specific pipe length/lift/charge and condensate arrangements. Extra refrigerant does not authorise exceeding limits. Window-AC condensate/slinger design is model-specific; no drilling or improvised pads. |
| Water heater | Actual supply, load, phase, minimum dynamic flow/pressure and user demand. Do not claim all instantaneous heaters require three-phase. Gas isolation, flues and reconnection require registered gas trades; retain the storage-heater outlet compatibility hard stop. |
| Hood and cabinetry | Select before fabrication: mounting/substrate, duct route, penetrations, terminal weather protection, airflow and service access. Distinguish rain through a wall joint from wind-driven ingress through a duct. Never remove specified brackets/clearances to conceal a gap. Name the installer and warranty interface. |
| Washer, dryer and compact kitchen | Check door opening, loading, removal, isolation and connections as well as cavity size. Remove an appliance top only where its manufacturer permits. Compare wash and dry capacities separately; identify vented/condensing/heat-pump or gas requirements. Approve stacking kits. Plan landing space, rice-cooker steam, bins and sink workflow with actual users. |
| Boiling/chilled-water tap | Coordinate tank, filters, optional chiller/CO2, ventilation, electrical supply, isolation and replacement path under the sink. Do not convert sales claims into sterilisation or infant-feeding advice. |
| Imported/component-only kitchen | Confirm support, cutouts, finished measurements, connection compatibility, local installer responsibility, spares and warranty before purchase. A scratch/stain demonstration does not establish service-life performance. |
| Prefabricated unit bathroom | Check delivery and assembled envelopes, weight, floor levels, drainage, penetrations, maintenance access and responsibility at module/building interfaces. Do not omit building waterproofing on a 'never leaks' claim, or describe the module as an earthquake shelter. |

Provide drying space with load-rated rails, drip management and ventilation; do not recommend a portable dehumidifier inside a shower. Bathroom electrical placement requires the applicable zone/protection assessment, not simply a waterproof socket label. No universal pressure-test figure, duration or allowable drop is adopted from these extracts; use the site's [stage-inspection checks](../subskills/interior-site-supervision/references/residential-stage-inspections.md), a competent trade's documented method and equipment limits, including for partial renovations.

For niches, traps and drainage, verify levels, access and approved routing; never chase a structural slab/rebar to obtain depth. A lining may provide a recess subject to build-up and waterproofing review. Silicone around a pipe is not a substitute for an approved trap/connection. As-built records guide later fixing but do not prove a location safe to drill.

## Required doors remain a compliance gate

Sources 25, 89, 91, 101. Determine required ratings and approved kitchen arrangement before choosing PD, sliding or glazed doors. Private self-use does not waive required fire separation. Check the complete tested doorset and installation, not glass percentage or a generic BS/EN label. Bathroom ventilation/privacy details do not authorise adding a louvre to a rated door. Coordinate threshold sections, both floor levels, accessibility, air transfer and water containment; there is no universal frame-first or stone-first sequence. See [BD alteration FAQ](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/faq/index_alteration_and_addition_works_in_domestic_premises.html). Halt noncompliant removal and obtain the appropriate professional/approval route.

## Follow-up batch: dishwasher, waterproofing records and entrance doors

[Follow-up audit](../../Reference/TIM-HO-BATCH2-COVERAGE-2026-11-08.md), indices 1, 3, 5, 7, 11. Countertop dishwashers preserve cabinet storage but consume preparation/landing area; test the actual workflow and door opening. Confirm model-specific water, drain and electrical needs explicitly in the quote, not a universal 400 mm landing-space minimum.

Kitchen waterproofing must be reviewed as a complete containment/drainage/interface design, including open-plan thresholds; a generic 300 mm upstand is not a design and cannot contain water escaping into the living area. Do not adopt claims that pipe-bending practice guarantees no future leaks.

A missing waterproofing-stage photo is an evidence gap. Hold concealment, recover batch/application/inspection records, and agree a competent inspection/test or compatible remedial method. A second photograph, darker colour, unequal coating heights, empty product tub or hourly spraying does not establish coat count, thickness, continuity or watertightness. Do not blindly add a coat: confirm the specified system, recoat window and cure. Test scope, load, duration and acceptance need a documented approved method; visual flow to a drain does not prove absence of leaks below.

For entrance-door refurbishment/replacement, check the complete required rated assembly, approved finish/hardware changes and DMC/management requirements. Matching the exterior appearance is not permission to conceal replacement from management; unchanged neighbours’ doors do not establish a legal prohibition either. A two-colour design is only an option within those constraints. No stripping/re-facing or site solvent spraying is authorised by the video. Inspect new-flat defects before strip-out and preserve evidence; intended renovation does not remove the value of inspection. Fridge fit or a floor-level difference does not authorise removing kitchen fire separation or prescribing a fixed screed build-up.
