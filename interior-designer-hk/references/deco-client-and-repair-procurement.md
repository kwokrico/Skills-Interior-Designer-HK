# Client coordination and repair procurement

Source: DECO-TV 天使與魔鬼 and 裝修佬伏線; [inventory](../../Reference/DECO-TV-COLLECTION-2026-11-07.md). Contractor perspectives and reported disputes are not findings of liability or universal market rules. Use existing HKRG tender, variation, professional-practice and handover guidance.

- Agree a household brief, budget, decision maker, drawing scope/fee, revision allowance and approval dates. Offer a common measured scope to bidders; seek comparable quotations without disclosing a competitor's confidential pricing. Separate meetings can aid discussion, but joint tender briefings and competitive bids are legitimate. Respectful communication does not require accepting unsupported technical claims.
- Define delivery receipt separately from product inspection: identify who checks model, dimensions, quantity, damage and finish against the order, who records exceptions and who contacts the supplier within applicable deadlines. Do not assume the person signing delivery accepted quality or that responsibility always belongs to the owner.
- Retained furniture needs an inventory, condition photos, protection, movement/storage responsibilities and priced programme impact. For occupied renovation assess safe separation, dust/noise, access, essential services and emergency routes; arrange alternative accommodation where safe occupation cannot be maintained. Do not infer insurance cover or exclusions from the video.
- With separate trade contracts assign a coordinator, predecessor-release gates, access dates, protection and damage-reporting responsibility. Record work condition before each handoff. Clarify any paid coordination service. Weather/programme float and alternative accommodation plans help planning but do not automatically establish an extension-of-time entitlement.
- Treat “just help install this” as a scope/change request: agree responsibility, cost and programme before proceeding. Free work does not automatically remove workmanship responsibilities. Report defects promptly without suggesting late discovery loses all rights; use actual notice/payment terms. A suggested 20% retention is neither a universal requirement nor inherently invalid.
- Repairs: identify the legal contracting party, trading name, payee and relevant personnel; confirm diagnostic fee, scope, units, expected quantities, total or capped authority, exclusions and written approval before exceeding it. A low estimate paired with unlimited per-weight chemical charges is an unresolved cost exposure. Company registration, local presence, followers and platform reviews do not guarantee competence.
- Request a location-specific diagnosis: symptoms, inspection method, instrument identity/calibration where relevant, actual images/readings, dimensions, limitations and proposed remedy. Implausible readings or generic reports need clarification; they do not by themselves establish fraud. Compare genuinely independent providers and channels, not merely several advertisements.
- Before replacement AC work establish old-line compatibility/condition and priced replacement options. Before takeover of unfinished works preserve photos, drawings, tests, paid/unpaid inventory and defects; assess safety, permissions, ownership/access and actual contract termination/notice arrangements with qualified advice when disputed. The successor must state retained-work assumptions, investigation, remedy scope and warranty boundaries. Avoid indefinite delay to urgent safety work while liability is debated.
- Do not adopt purported case prices, fines, cancellation rights, accusations, guaranteed recoveries or “always trust the tradesperson” claims. Independent competent review remains appropriate. Written agreements and due diligence complement one another.
- Open-kitchen sprinkler incident: coordinate hob/hood heat sources with the approved fire-system layout and responsible fire contractor/designer before ceiling closure. Preserve records of changes and investigate repeated cover release or activation promptly. Do not relocate, shield, disable or change sprinkler temperature ratings based on the video; use the existing fire-safety escalation path.
