# Mall residential design and product evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-10. Use with the owning modules, not as installation or statutory approval. [Per-file source audit](../../Reference/MALL-COVERAGE-2026-11-10.md) links archived extracts and records exclusions. These are supplied captions/ASR transcripts, not inspected drawings, original video playback or product test evidence. Source numbers below are the audit's zero-based IDs. Publication dates are unknown.

## Storage and moving furniture

Extend [furnishing and window-treatment planning](ching-furnishings-and-window-treatments.md) and [joinery/home-use checks](tim-ho-finishes-and-home-use.md).

| Item | Brief and drawing checks | Release evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Shoe cabinet (0, 37) | Count by user, shoe type and actual pair size; test tall boots, angled shelves and pull-outs with samples. Adjustable shelves must suit the chosen runner/hinge geometry. Separate wet-shoe airing, everyday access and long-term storage; include a removable cleanable tray where appropriate. | Actual internal clear dimensions, door/entrance swept paths, cleaning access and designed supports for floating units; no universal depth or odour-free promise. |
| Fold-down wall seat, extending table, pull-out surface (2, 33, 37, 39) | Draw occupied and stowed states, chairs, legs, leaf storage, locking/release and pinch zones. A fold-down seat needs support for occupied use, not merely its own weight. | Rated complete mechanism, substrate/fixing/load path, safe retention and replacement access. Do not infer hot-appliance suitability from a pull-out demonstration. |
| Parallel-opening cabinet door / corner storage (36) | Plot the complete hardware trajectory against adjacent fridge doors and people, not just the open end position. Check reach and retrieval of corner contents. | Manufacturer geometry, permitted door mass/size and adjustment/service access. |
| Robot or wheeled appliance bay (10, 37, 47) | Allow actual robot height, dock approach, turning, cable routing and service removal. Distinguish a storage parking bay from a permitted operating enclosure for a dehumidifier or hot appliance. | Model-specific operating air path, clearances, power and service access; drawer runners or wheels do not establish safe operation. |

Expand the appliance/furniture schedule (8, 9, 43) to include retained sofas, aquariums, strollers, luggage, gym equipment, shrines and robot docks. Record model, measured dimensions, full operating weight, moving/maintenance envelope, supply needs and responsible supplier. Cultural display preferences do not establish fire-safe flame clearances. A download mentioned in a caption is not a reviewed schedule.

Layout case studies (9–16, 43–47) are briefing examples. Label gross, saleable and net usable areas separately; do not reuse illustrated widths as minimums. Keep future access needs in the brief: present occupants being young is not justification to reduce an accessible doorway. Existing compliance routes govern wall changes, wet areas, open kitchens, helper accommodation and lofts. Borrowed light or an internal opening does not establish statutory daylight/ventilation compliance; a fixed pane supplies no ventilation. For opening interior windows, show effective clear opening, leaf sweep/retention, pinch zones, privacy and glass replacement access.

## Basins, sinks and worktops

Use [sanitaryware selection and rough-in](../subskills/interior-material-procurement/references/sanitaryware-selection-and-rough-in.md) and [countertop fabrication](../subskills/interior-material-procurement/references/countertop-selection-and-fabrication.md) first.

- Basin names must match the section: above-counter, rimmed inset, undermount, semi-recessed, integrated or wall-hung (17, 27). Inset and undermount are not interchangeable. Coordinate finished **rim height**, tap reach and splash test with the actual user; cabinet height or a video basin-depth figure is insufficient. Specify whether the basin has an overflow and a matching waste arrangement; retain trap access around U-shaped drawers. Integrated appearance alone does not determine whether repairs require replacement of the whole worktop.
- Sink accessories (56): test the actual wok/pans and dirty-to-clean workflow. Check removable drainer/board stability, loading, cleaning and storage. An accessory does not prevent drainage blockage or guarantee a percentage improvement in usability.
- Natural marble/granite and stainless worktops (18): obtain the actual material/finish, stain and cleaner compatibility, sealing requirements where applicable, fabrication/joint/support details and repair method. Do not infer maintenance-free performance, direct hot-pan tolerance, food hygiene or scratch resistance from the generic material or a stainless grade alone.

## Product labels and demonstration limits

| Claim in extracts | Qualified specification response |
|---|---|
| Magnetic backsplash proves enamel (19, 64) | Magnet attraction alone does not identify vitreous enamel. Obtain face/coating and substrate identification, edge/cut-out treatment, fixing/adhesive system and applicable heat/cleaner evidence. Painted steel and other magnetic assemblies need their own evidence. |
| Glass backsplash or overlay is heatproof (19) | Coordinate holes/cut-outs before manufacture as required by the selected glass process. Check hob adjacency and the entire substrate/adhesive/finish system; retained tiles must be sound. Do not reconstruct an unintelligible product name from ASR. |
| Particleboard equals low-density fibreboard (31) | Specify particleboard and fibreboard as distinct product families; identify actual core, grade, thickness, face and test method. Melamine facing does not make the core waterproof or establish a fire rating. See [joinery methods/emissions](../subskills/interior-millwork-technical/references/joinery-method-and-emissions.md). |
| GREENGUARD Gold means every chemical is absent (65) | UL describes Gold as low chemical-emission certification with additional limits and CDPH chamber-method compliance. Verify the exact certified product and scope; distinguish emissions from chemical content. It is not a zero-emission promise, installed-room test or diagnosis of a child's health. [UL certification scope](https://www.ul.com/services/ul-greenguard-certification), checked 2026-11-10. |
| Immersion, knife or flame demonstration proves wallpaper/cloth performance (32) | Require evidence applicable to the installed system, substrate, moisture conditions and specified fire performance. Do not repeat improvised burn tests or infer mould resistance from a sample demonstration. |
| 38/40/50 window label establishes thickness or quality (76) | Request the actual profile series, section drawing, wall thickness, glazing, hardware and complete system evidence. Do not treat the series number as glass thickness or a universal wall-thickness specification. Overlay frames also affect clear opening, drainage and retained-frame condition. Neighbours' windows are not approval evidence. |

For typhoon tape/film claims (59), distinguish precaution from certified glazing performance. HKO's [precautions](https://www.hko.gov.hk/tc/informtc/precaution.htm) include tape strips on large exposed panes to reduce injury if glass breaks; that is not evidence of wind-strengthening or hurricane-proof film. Follow current HKO precautions and the competent window professional's assessment. Do not endorse a product from the extract alone.

## Window coverings, privacy glass and finishes

- Curtains (4, 26): coordinate pleat/S-wave stack depth, corner meeting, pelmets, cleaning access and AC obstruction against the actual system. Obtain the manufacturer's feasibility for shaped cut-outs or use coordinated separate panels. A pelmet may reduce head light leakage; it does not establish whole-room blackout. Avoid universal recess depths, fitting deductions or sound-insulation promises.
- Switchable privacy glass (33): obscuring a view does not necessarily block light. Establish the selected product's powered/unpowered states, controller location, replacement access and safe wiring across a moving leaf. Privacy glazing is not automatically a fire-rated door assembly. Coordinate with MEP and fire/life-safety modules before release.
- Flooring and tiles (23, 28, 58, 66): include joint materials and edge profiles in the quotation. Separate grouted joints from designed movement joints; “seamless” appearance is not permission to omit movement provision. Timber refinishing depends on the actual wear layer and finish; do not prescribe blanket waxing. Procurement waste follows layout/cuts/batch and pack rounding, not a fixed promotional allowance.
- Lighting/electrical drawings (40, 71, 74, 79): use the drawing legend and reconciled point schedule. A high-load appliance shown on the same sheet is not assigned to the lighting circuit by that fact. CCT is not brightness, and a fixture count is not an illuminance calculation. Retain existing registered-trade, load and bathroom-zone gates.

Existing [finish evidence and maintenance](deco-finish-evidence-and-maintenance.md), [services/smart home](tim-ho-services-and-smart-home.md) and [project coordination](tim-ho-project-coordination.md) cover cleaning, damp diagnosis, lift beds, locks, fan/AC installation, quotes and handover. Reject unverified chemical recipes, natural-equals-safe claims, health causality, current-price promises and automatic approval from a video. Peeling paint or efflorescence alone does not prove deep mould. Colour ratios and Japanese-style labels can support a client moodboard, but are not mandatory rules or health treatments; the source's colour-of-year claim is not adopted.
