# Contractor fit, measured scope and disputed work

Field sources: DECO-TV 裝修中伏研習班, 天使與魔鬼, 天生_裝修佬 and 裝修全接觸, reviewed 2026-11-07. [Per-file coverage and archived extracts](../../Reference/DECO-TV-BATCH2-COVERAGE-2026-11-07.md). Read alongside [contractor due diligence](../subskills/interior-tendering-qa/references/contractor-due-diligence.md), [quotation field traps](../subskills/interior-tendering-qa/references/quotation-field-traps.md) and the actual adopted contract. These are conditional design/administration checks, not findings about the reported parties or legal entitlements.

## Match evidence to the service required

Sources: `EQShDEeU1kE`, `RPUAEsU0M-A`, `H3j3FwUKWfE`, `v2OUF7jisV8`, `0b5qL9B3BPY`, `THR3t_1kU5Q`, `vcnxzJbjGjg`, `x9JEvMJrLio`, `OVlAPGBeQqw`, `NpdpatXx5pY`, `Wwb8KsVhqWg` (Summary / Transcript).

- Separate mandatory safety, competent execution and contractual quality from preferences in style, communication, shopping assistance and revision service. Rank the latter against the client's brief; do not treat lower price or higher client “tolerance” as permission for defective or unsafe work.
- Before seeking bids, identify budget basis, scope difficulty, furniture preferences, drawing/revision needs, desired site involvement, acceptance evidence and aftercare expectations. Use a completed comparable project and proposed team's record to test capability. A village house and garden needs relevant experience beyond minor flat touch-ups; a television portfolio does not prove construction competence.
- Ask who actually supplied the design/lighting/material advice in the showcased job. For unusual details, request method, constraints, similar completed work and coordination responsibility. A separate designer and contractor can work well when information, interfaces and fees are defined; their separation is not proof of a saving or failure.
- Agree contact person, decision deadlines, progress reports, safe site visits and inspection hold points. One satisfactory answer is not evidence that nine unexamined technical issues are correct. A contractor's familiar method remains subject to specification, system compatibility and compliance; discuss alternatives before award without accepting an unsuitable method as “local practice”.
- Inspect completed work with permission and agree representative samples, viewing conditions and measurable acceptance criteria. A showroom is not a guarantee of concealed quality. Price is not proof of quality, and refusal of access to an occupied client's home alone does not prove poor workmanship.
- For an adviser/platform, identify appointment, fees/commissions, conflicts, inspection scope, document checks, escalation powers and whether it guarantees anything in writing. Referral, membership, self-described neutrality, reviews or a charter logo do not establish independence or completion insurance. Retain legal identity, contact and address evidence.

## Make quantity and inclusion boundaries visible

Sources: `zA1GjhmUmMY`, `-15KHOlrbbA`, `LQKKnjfvsuY`, `klSVIjKH6jM` (00:30–03:42 caption locator), `MvP2lkJqZKw`.

| Scope | Resolve before comparing prices |
|---|---|
| Joinery “per foot” | Unit and measured axis, height/depth, cabinet runs, appliance voids, corner treatment, carcass/finish, hardware, backing/support panels, exposed ends, scribes/fillers, delivery and installation. Record drawings/revisions and priced exclusions. |
| Appliance opening within a cabinet run | Draw the remaining panels, framing and countertop supports; an empty appliance bay is neither automatically free nor automatically chargeable as a full cabinet. Agree the measurement rule and any omission credit. |
| Special hardware | List baskets, corner mechanisms, thin drawers and other extras separately; a video's “normal” versus “extra” distinction is not a binding pricing rule. |
| Whole-flat pipe replacement | Mark start/end points and retained sections on a drawing, including meter/valve-to-flat corridor runs, common-area access, ceiling opening, isolation, approvals, testing and making good. The phrase alone does not establish inclusion. |
| Hidden demolition/substrate conditions | Record survey limitations, suspected extra tile/coating layers, provisional scope, rates or approval cap, evidence and programme consequences. Do not presume all hidden work is free or always the owner's cost. |

Use one common scope/quantity schedule to compare bids, with exclusions and provisional sums visible. A single wardrobe rate can screen a budget conversation only; it cannot predict a whole-flat price. Historical prices, “20% for scribing” and market percentages are not estimating defaults. Structural spalling discovered during stripping follows the existing AP/RSE halt, not an ordinary extra-work instruction.

## Programme, variations and approval records

Sources: `-0dj1hDrIHs`, `hvZu_UOQRWQ`, `nkVPF-OVHk4`, `f-SLL-Ncolk`, `UxtrDdeKiRc`, `nlPejgk60w0`, `Pn-2rL4iy1Y`, `klSVIjKH6jM`.

- Obtain actual factory order deadlines, holiday closures/reopening, transport and installation dates, with float and dependencies. Do not assume every factory reopens after the Lantern Festival or that a pre-holiday deposit guarantees a lower price.
- When a trade is absent, compare actual progress with the programme, material delivery and next release gate; seek an updated recovery plan. Absence alone proves neither abandonment nor acceptable delay.
- Record a delay when identified: cause, evidence, affected activity, critical dates, mitigation, notice and requested time/cost effect. Customs/weather delays and owner decisions do not automatically excuse or penalise either party. Assess the contract and causal effect; keep programme float separate from entitlement.
- Distinguish client approval of appearance/function from the assigned responsibility for site measurement, technical design, fabrication and checking. Keep approved revision, measured datum, explanations, changes and actual discrepancy. A client's signature alone does not establish who caused or must pay for an error.
- Record the condition of finishes and protection before and after each separately contracted trade's entry. Log damaged protection and restore it promptly. Do not allocate subsequent floor damage automatically to the main contractor, owner, third party or a 50/50 split without evidence and contract review.

## Defect evidence, takeover and dispute boundaries

Sources: `PmHOlTA9cv0`, `sKSFdhdXUls`, `p8nmjcNWBSA`, `R9XLyQOyNJY`, `aLoq_8--0-A`, `BzO4bcQjCa0`, `a533fimOsxI`, `9-ep1VoNtEY`, `Q4IF-OFiIvI`, `LQi7THSDh3k`, `klSVIjKH6jM`.

- For a cracked countertop, preserve crack photographs/location, slab/product and care evidence, fabrication drawing, cutouts/seams, supports/level, cabinet movement, installation and use history. Protect unsafe areas and obtain competent assessment. Material, fabrication, support, thermal exposure or impact are hypotheses, not proof of user misuse. Separate replacement material, removal/installation, protection and reinstatement costs. No automatic thirds, 2/3 or 3/4 liability formula, nor automatic transfer of a failed supplier's share.
- Keep payment entitlement, incomplete work, defects and termination separate. Review the actual milestones and notice provisions; do not advise unilateral withholding, early payment, lockout or termination from a video. Before a successor starts, document condition, safety, access, inventories, investigations, completion/remedial scope and warranty boundaries with qualified contract advice where disputed.
- Inventory and protect a contractor's abandoned tools; arrange a documented retrieval process with qualified advice on notice, access, storage and disposal rights. Passing the planned completion date or issuing seven days' notice does **not by itself** authorise disposal. Do not promise that filing a claim stops debt collection or guarantees recovery. Record threats/damage and use appropriate authorities rather than confrontation.
- The video's 裝修約章 is a private expectation/communication framework, distinct from HKRG and HKEDCA. Obtain the actual document/version, discuss disagreements and incorporate only expressly agreed terms with legal review. A video description is not the charter text, a statutory code, accreditation or a substitute for contract terms.
- Distinguish negotiation, mediation, arbitration, civil recovery and reporting suspected wrongdoing. Do not present criminal reporting and civil recovery as mutually exclusive. Assess settlement/release wording with qualified advice; no guaranteed timetable, cost, result or blanket “arbitration cannot be challenged” claim.

Authoritative checks on 2026-11-07: the [Judiciary's Small Claims Tribunal guide](https://www.judiciary.hk/en/court_services_facilities/sc.html) gives a monetary jurisdiction of HK$75,000 or less, not the videos' historic HK$50,000; do not split a larger claim to fit. Check current eligibility, fees and procedures when needed. The [Department of Justice arbitration overview](https://www.doj.gov.hk/en/legal_dispute/arbitration.html) includes court proceedings to set aside/enforce awards, so the video's absolute finality claim is not adopted. The [Consumer Council's 2024 renovation study](https://www.consumer.org.hk/en/advocacy/study-report/home_renovation_study) recommends standard quotations, accreditation, dispute resolution and education; a recommendation is not proof of enactment or endorsement of a particular platform.
