# Drawing review and measured quantities

Source review: [47-file audit and archived extracts](../../Reference/MULTI-SOURCE-COVERAGE-2026-11-09.md), zero-based indices 0–25 (Mr. Ken collection; 25 is Sun-Renovation). These are noisy transcripts of illustrated lessons, not the drawings themselves. Do not infer unseen dimensions or adopt example rates, assemblies or statutory claims. Read with [tender measurement rules](../subskills/interior-tendering-qa/references/hkedca-measurement-rules-summary.md), [mini tender set](cases/hk-residential-mini-tender-set.md) and [trade coordination](tim-ho-project-coordination.md).

## Survey, drawing purpose and issue control

Sources 3–4, 11, 14–15, 20, 22: preserve the dated measured-existing drawing as a baseline; develop proposed and demolition drawings as separate controlled issues. Record survey method, datum/units, photos, inaccessible areas and dimensions awaiting confirmation. Capture demise/common-area boundaries, service entry points, beams/soffits, openings/sills and delivery routes. Revisit to close specific gaps; neither a fixed visit count nor an arbitrary percentage surveyed is a release criterion.

Read the sheet purpose/status (concept, tender, approval or construction), revision, legend and linked schedules before measuring. A line missing from the proposal does not authorise demolition. Overlay aligned revisions to distinguish retained/new/removed partitions, doors, glazing and operable partitions; identify required elevations/sections and specialist drawings. Register discrepancies as RFIs with an owner, response date and affected release hold. Do not deliberately distort dimensions or silently shrink joinery to resolve a mismatch.

Coordinate layout, furniture, partition, power/ELV, RCP, finishes and elevations iteratively. Plan symbols alone do not establish outlet height, overhead obstruction, fixing support or service access. State SFL/FFL and datum meanings explicitly; no assumed floor build-up. Compliance review starts with the brief/layout and continues through revisions. Never submit one occupancy/furniture arrangement and install a different non-compliant arrangement afterwards.

## Quantity worksheet and checks

Sources 2, 6, 9: record drawing/revision, element ID, finish/assembly, dimensions and units, measurement rule, deductions, measured quantity, allowance and pricing basis. Keep traced lengths/closed areas identifiable; check scale, duplicate lines, polygon closure and a known dimension before accepting CAD totals. A downloadable script is not required to establish quantities.

| Work | Measure and reconcile |
|---|---|
| Partitions | Split runs by actual height and assembly. Separate full-height walls from bands above doors/glazing; apply the agreed opening deductions. Distinguish partition area from board procurement: faces, layers, thickness and insulation are explicit. |
| Paint | Measure actual finished wall faces, ceiling faces, beam sides, bulkhead drops and returns by finish. Check whether beam soffits are already included in the ceiling projection before adding them. Agree deductions and treatment of narrow surfaces; do not use floor area as universal paint area. |
| Floors | Separate each finish zone, skirting, thresholds and transitions. Verify substrate/build-up and finished levels; a metal transition strip alone does not resolve accessibility or trip risk. |
| Doors/glass/movable partitions | Reconcile plan IDs with schedules, opening sizes, operation, complete rated/acoustic assemblies and hardware; do not count them again as ordinary partition work. |

Conversions: mm to m = divide by 1,000; mm² to m² = divide by 1,000,000; m² to ft² ≈ multiply by 10.7639. Keep precision until final reporting. For example, a 4 m × 3 m wall is 12 m² of partition before deductions; two faces with one board layer each require 24 m² of board before openings/waste, not 24 m² of partition.

Separate net measured work, contractual chargeable quantity and procurement quantity. Add documented cutting/layout waste and pack rounding only to the relevant basis; do not silently mark up dimensions. Example: 23 m² net, an explicitly agreed 8% procurement allowance and 2 m² packs give 24.84 m² before rounding, hence 13 packs/26 m² purchased. This does not automatically make 26 m² chargeable work.

Compare rates only for the same assembly, measured scope and exclusions. Cost markup and sales margin differ: price = cost × (1 + markup); price = cost ÷ (1 − margin). A 30% markup yields about 23.1% margin; neither percentage is a recommended fee or universal allowance.

## Functional services and scope reconciliation

Sources 1, 5–8, 10, 12, 18, 21–23: an RCP shows visible coordination, not all building-services design. Reconcile supply, return, fresh air and exhaust using verified legends/system information. Include actual refrigerant/chilled-water interfaces, condensate route/fall or specified pump, insulation, power/isolation, control zone and service/replacement access. A return grille is not automatically outdoor ventilation. Thermostat location must represent the served zone and follow system guidance, not blindly sit in a supply jet. No universal drain gradient, cassette airflow multiplier or assumption that every central system uses chilled water.

Trace AV/data/security functions end to end: user device → interface/control → cable/network/distribution equipment → destination → power and commissioning test. Confirm actual connectors and equipment, not universal VGA/analogue telephone requirements. PoE devices still need compatible power sourcing and a checked power budget. Reconcile socket quantities and heights with equipment/furniture; do not infer circuit capacity from outlet count. Access-control release and power arrangements must preserve compliant escape.

Show containment/support envelopes, penetrations and access zones, including hangers and maintenance reach. Allocate design, supply, installation, testing, certification and making-good responsibility for every interface. Distinguish retained equipment cleaning/relocation from new installation, owner-supplied furniture relocation/assembly, whiteboard/screen backing and mounting, and joinery transport sections. A specialist's separate contract does not remove the coordinator's interface checks or make fire services optional.

## Limits on adopting the demonstrations

Sources 0, 13, 16–17, 24–25 largely reinforce existing material, joinery, statutory and planning modules. Wall tapping, apparent thickness or a trial hole do not establish permission to cut/chase/remove structure. Use the AP/RSE and approved-record route. Rated partitions are complete assemblies; substituting plywood for plasterboard is not automatically equivalent. Concealment cannot cure damp, unsafe services or inaccessible valves. Lease reinstatement follows the actual agreement and handover records.

The small-flat example is a functional option, not a dimensioned template: confirm occupancy, children's changing study/storage needs, window daylight/ventilation, privacy, intercom access and appliance clearances. Do not block a window because neighbours overlook it, or move kitchen/bathroom partitions and plumbing without the statutory/estate and service checks. Use [compliance hard stops](compliance.md) and [credential checks](trade-credentials-and-service-evidence.md) for unsafe electrical, fire, water or window claims.
