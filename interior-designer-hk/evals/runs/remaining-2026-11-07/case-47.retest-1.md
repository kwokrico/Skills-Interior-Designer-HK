# Actual answer

**Compliance gap memo — layout issue on hold**  
**Project:** HK living-room subdivision into two bedrooms  
**Review date:** 7 November 2026  
**Status:** Advisory review; not for construction

The proposed inner bedroom cannot be treated as compliant on the strength of glass above the partition and a split AC. I cannot issue this arrangement as a construction layout. No measured plan, approved building records or room-by-room daylight and ventilation evidence has been provided.

| Item | Finding | Required correction / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Natural light | Internal high-level glass provides borrowed light; it does not by itself demonstrate the bedroom's required natural-light provision. | Map the external openings serving each proposed room, with room areas, window dimensions, external obstructions and approved arrangements. |
| Ventilation | A normal split AC cools and recirculates room air; it does not establish outdoor-air supply or natural ventilation. Fixed glass provides no ventilation opening. | Assess natural ventilation separately from cooling; document any proposed outdoor-air system and its lawful approval basis. |
| Electric lighting | Added luminaires can improve usable illumination but do not resolve the statutory daylight question. | After layout feasibility is established, design ambient/task lighting, glare control and accessible drivers independently. |
| Escape and services | New partitions and bedroom doors may change escape routes and any existing detector/sprinkler coverage. | Check access to the flat exit, door swings, kitchen separation and applicable fire-service coverage. Coordinate AC pipework, condensate drainage, power and maintenance access. |

The relevant framework is the **Building (Planning) Regulations, regulations 30–32**. BD's **APP-130, paragraphs 1–3**, describes an alternative performance-based natural-lighting and ventilation route, requiring substantiated assessment and applications to modify prescriptive requirements. It is not automatic permission to replace a bedroom window with borrowed light and cooling. An Authorized Person (AP) must establish whether an alternative route is applicable and obtain the necessary Building Authority acceptance. [BD APP-130, December 2016 revision, checked 7 November 2026](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP130.pdf).

The remediated options are:

1. **Re-plan both bedrooms along the external-window frontage**, only if each can meet the applicable natural-light, ventilation and escape requirements. The remaining living space must also be checked.
2. **Retain a single compliant bedroom arrangement** if the frontage cannot support two. Any internal storage/circulation area must have a genuine non-habitable use; calling a sleeping room a “store” does not cure the problem.
3. **Have an AP assess a substantiated alternative scheme before further design.** Acceptance is uncertain; mechanical ventilation alone should not be assumed to make the current proposal acceptable.

To prepare a reviewable 1:50 test-fit, provide a dimensioned existing plan and approved layout, external-window sizes and opening types, room/ceiling heights, external obstruction information, intended occupants, entrance/kitchen locations and existing services. The AP should confirm the statutory route; estate consent is a separate requirement. Release remains on hold until the critical evidence and applicable approvals are resolved.
