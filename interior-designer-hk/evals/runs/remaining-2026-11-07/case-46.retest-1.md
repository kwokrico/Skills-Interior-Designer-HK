# Actual answer

**Hold the platform proposal.** Keeping the anchors unchanged does not preserve the barrier's effective height: the surface people stand on changes.

| Datum / check | Height |
|---|---:|
| Barrier top above original slab | 1100 mm |
| Proposed finished platform above that slab | 200 mm |
| Effective barrier height above the platform | **1100 − 200 = 900 mm** |
| Shortfall against the HK 1100 mm barrier benchmark | **200 mm** |

For a Hong Kong project, BD's **APP-110, paragraphs 2–3**, gives a minimum 1.1 m barrier height measured from the adjoining finished surface people can step on, with specific curb/step conditions. A usable platform beside the barrier must be assessed as that new datum; it cannot simply retain the original slab datum. Platform width and its exact relationship to the barrier still need a measured section. [BD APP-110](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/signed/APP110se.pdf).

**Do not construct this arrangement as described.** This is a fall-safety hold under the skill's [compliance rules](C:/Users/Rico/PycharmProjects/Skills-Interior%20Designer-HK/interior-designer-hk/references/compliance.md), not an anchor-alteration question.

Remediated options are:

- **Omit the platform beside the barrier** and verify the retained barrier height from the actual existing finished surface. A height measured from bare slab does not establish existing compliance either.
- **Retain the platform only with a professionally assessed barrier solution.** An Authorized Person / Registered Structural Engineer (AP/RSE) should assess the complete barrier and support arrangement. A top level of 1300 mm above the original slab is the arithmetic level needed to provide 1100 mm over a +200 mm platform, but it is not an approved extension detail or permission to reuse existing anchors.

Before releasing a revised design, obtain a measured plan/section and confirm the jurisdiction, approved existing arrangement, platform extent/build-up and barrier type. Have the AP/RSE check climbability, openings, barrier load path, and the platform's added dead/imposed loads on the balcony. Coordinate waterproofing continuity, upstands, drainage falls, outlet access and threshold transitions so the platform does not trap water or obstruct drainage. Establish **estate/management consent and the applicable statutory approval route separately**; management consent does not replace statutory requirements. No anchor or glass modification detail should be issued before that assessment.
