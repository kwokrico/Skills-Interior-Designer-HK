**A meets the deadline; B does not.** Under Hong Kong EMSD’s published transition to the *Code of Practice for the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations, 2025 Edition*, both completion **and connection to electricity supply** must occur on or before **31 December 2026** for the 2020-edition grace provision. Code 1 also requires compliance with the relevant electricity supplier’s Supply Rules. [EMSD publication index](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/en/electricity_safety/publications/codes_of_practice/index.html); [2025 CoP, Code 1, printed p.12](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_443/COP_E_2025.pdf).
