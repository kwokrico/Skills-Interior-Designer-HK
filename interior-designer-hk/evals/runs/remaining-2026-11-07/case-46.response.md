**Hold the proposed platform: the unchanged barrier would provide only 900 mm of protection above the new standing surface.**

| Height check | Result |
|---|---:|
| Barrier top above original slab | 1100 mm |
| New finished platform above original slab | 200 mm |
| Effective barrier height above platform | **1100 − 200 = 900 mm** |
| Shortfall against the HK 1100 mm criterion | **200 mm** |

For a Hong Kong balcony, Buildings Department **APP-110, paragraphs 2–3**, requires a barrier height of at least 1.1 m and measures it from the adjoining finished surface people can step on. The platform changes that datum. Keeping the anchors unchanged does **not** preserve the effective height. The limited curb exceptions are not a basis for treating an ordinary usable platform as the original slab level. [BD APP-110](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/signed/APP110se.pdf)

Proceed only with a remediated scheme:

- **Omit the raised platform beside the barrier**, then verify height from the actual finished balcony surface, including all existing finishes.
- **Retain the platform only with a professionally reviewed barrier solution.** Geometrically, its top would need to be at least **1300 mm above the original slab** to give 1100 mm above this platform. That is a height target, not an instruction to bolt on an extension.

Have the Authorized Person / Registered Structural Engineer verify the measured section, platform extent, approved arrangement, barrier assembly and approval route before work. Include platform loading, drainage/waterproofing, openings, solid base and climbability in that review. Do not fabricate barrier or fixing alterations from this height calculation.
