This holds the substitution under the frozen skill’s `references/compliance.md` life-safety and missing-critical-evidence rules. It is an evidence gap, not a finding that every US-tested product is prohibited.

**Critical information outstanding:** project jurisdiction; approved door schedule and fire strategy; door location/use; required fire and smoke performance; certificate/report and test-standard edition; exact proposed leaf, frame, seals and hardware; opening size and supporting wall construction. Hong Kong requirements below apply if this is an HK project; confirm the applicable approval basis before release.

| Review item | Evidence needed to release the hold |
|---|---|
| Required performance | Approved rating/classification and applicable integrity, insulation and smoke requirements, matched against the proposed evidence—not just a minute value. |
| Complete doorset | Full test/classification report and permitted field of application covering the exact leaf, frame, intumescent and smoke seals, hinges, closer, latch/lock, glazing and other fittings. | Installation interfaces | Coordinated detail and manufacturer’s instructions covering supporting wall, opening dimensions, frame anchorage, perimeter fire stopping, packing, head/jamb/threshold clearances, floor finish and permitted trimming. Generic sealant or arbitrary site gap adjustments cannot be assumed acceptable. |
| Traceability and approvals | Report authenticity, competent testing body, product/model identification, installation instructions and documented substitution acceptance by the responsible project professional; authority approval where required. |

For Hong Kong, BD’s **Fire Safety Code 2011 (2024 Edition)**, Part E, E5 addresses opening-protection testing and identification; E9 separately addresses smoke leakage. E1.3 provides a route for other standards through an Authorized Person demonstrating equivalence or non-inferiority in accordance with E16.2. A US certificate is therefore neither automatic acceptance nor automatic rejection. The precise standard, scope and applicable performance must be reviewed. [BD Fire Safety Code, Part E](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/fs_code2011.pdf) (checked 7 November 2026).

Proceed with one of these remediated procurement options:

1. Obtain the originally approved complete assembly from another verified source, with confirmed availability and traceability.
2. Submit an alternative complete tested doorset whose evidence covers its intended installation and required performance, for formal review before ordering or installation.
3. If retaining this leaf is proposed, obtain suitable evidence/assessment for the **entire proposed combination** and any required local acceptance. Keep the hold until that is resolved; otherwise replace the proposal.

Record actual lead times, cost differences and programme impacts for these options; none are established by the prompt. After approval, revise the door/material schedules and interface detail, then inspect the delivered set and installation, record clearances and concealed perimeter work, and verify closing/latching operation. Retain identification, reports and maintenance instructions for handover. Do not remove existing fire protection or leave an opening unprotected merely to maintain the programme.
