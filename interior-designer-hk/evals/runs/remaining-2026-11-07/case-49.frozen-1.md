# Historical grace-period comparison

**A meets the date condition for the 2020-code grace provision; B does not.** This concerns Hong Kong EMSD's Code of Practice for the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations.

The 2025 edition, Code 1 (printed p. 12), permits reliance on the 2020 edition where the installation or relevant part complies with that edition, both completion and connection to electricity supply occur on or before 31 December 2026, and the relevant electricity supplier's Supply Rules are satisfied. The deadline is inclusive and both events must occur by it. [EMSD 2025 CoP, Code 1](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_443/COP_E_2025.pdf).

| Installation | Completion | Supply connection | Historical audit result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 31 December 2026 | 31 December 2026 | Within the grace-period date condition. It can rely on the 2020 edition if its technical compliance and the supplier's Supply Rules are established. |
| B | 31 December 2026 | 1 January 2027 | Outside the grace provision: timely completion alone is insufficient. With no special approval, assess against the 2025 edition; 2020 compliance alone cannot establish compliance through this grace provision. |

This distinguishes eligibility, not overall electrical safety or certification. Missing the grace deadline does not itself prove that B's otherwise identical technical installation fails the 2025 edition. For the audit, retain completion and connection records and the applicable compliance/testing evidence. Do not replace these specified historical dates with the date of today's review. [EMSD published transition notice](https://wcms.emsd.gov.hk/m/en/electricity_safety/new_edition_cop/new_edition_cop_electricity_wiring_regulations/index.html).
