# Actual answer

Hue, value and saturation describe three different qualities of a colour:

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hue** | The colour family, such as red, yellow or blue. | A blue cushion has a **blue hue**. |
| **Value** | How light or dark a colour appears. | Pale blue has a **higher value** than dark navy blue. |
| **Saturation** | How vivid or muted a colour appears. | Vivid blue is **more saturated** than a greyish blue of similar lightness. |

A colour can therefore be described as **blue in hue, light in value and low in saturation**.
