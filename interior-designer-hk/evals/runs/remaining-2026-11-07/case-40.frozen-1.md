# Bedroom storage, window covering and future sofa-bed brief

**Status: design intent only; hold final dimensions and ordering pending measured coordination.** The book’s dimensions are illustrative design guidance, not Hong Kong legal minima. I would not specify deep shelves throughout: storage depth should follow your possessions, access frequency and usable circulation.

The critical gaps are the measured room and opening sizes, full inward sash/handle sweep, mounting substrate, storage inventory, users’ reach/mobility needs, and the future sofa-bed’s closed and fully extended dimensions. These prevent a final fit or compliance conclusion under the skill’s compliance rules for missing critical inputs.

| Element | Proposed brief | Evidence required before order |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday storage | Use shallower adjustable shelves for frequently retrieved items; keep items visible and reachable. Reserve deeper storage for measured bulky items. Consider drawers or pull-outs where deep retrieval would otherwise be difficult. | Inventory by item size, quantity, frequency and visibility; user reach trial; full drawer/door opening clearance. |
| Fixed shelving | Limit fixed units to zones that leave the future bed and window operation clear. Separate display shelving from bulk storage. | Site-measured elevations; shelf spans and intended loads; board/finish and edge details; verified support, fixing substrate and hardware capacities. Do not select fixings from a textbook sketch. |
| Blackout curtains | Retain fabric curtains as an option, using a tested blackout lining/fabric and a coordinated track, centre overlap, side returns and head treatment. Agree how dark the room must be and test edge/head light leakage. Blackout fabric alone does not establish a blackout room. | Fabric/lining sample, cleaning and replacement instructions, actual track loading and mounting detail; installation mock-up or sample bay. |
| Inward-opening window | Draw sash and handle movement in plan and section. Place the track and stacked fabric outside that operating envelope; allow curtains to retract fully for opening, cleaning and access to controls. Verify the head treatment does not catch the sash. | Reveal depth, head clearance, handle projection, full swing and curtain stack-back dimensions. Do not block the window or force the sash against fabric. |
| Future sofa bed | Reserve the complete extended-bed and conversion envelope now. Prefer movable/modular surrounding furniture so future conversion does not require demolition of storage. | Actual candidate model drawings, mechanism operation/pinch zones, wall offset, bedding storage and remaining route to the door/window in both seating and sleeping modes. Check packaged dimensions, lift/stair turns and delivery access. Trial seat comfort and bed access with the users. |

For the window brief, also confirm daytime privacy and view, night privacy, daylight/glare preferences, manual control reach, and whether children will use the room. Obtain the selected system’s current cord-safety and installation instructions. Any motorised option needs separate power, control and maintenance-access coordination before selection. Request product-specific evidence for any fire, acoustic or thermal claim; no blanket curtain performance claim is justified.

If the curtain stack cannot clear the inward sash, compare a room-side mounting arrangement with adequate stand-off and side parking space against a fully retractable shade system whose raised head and guides also clear the sash. Both remain conditional on measurement and the agreed darkening performance.

**HK requirements remain a separate check.** Preserve the window’s approved light and ventilation provision; do not treat electric lighting or air conditioning as automatic substitutes. BD’s APP-130 provides a performance-based alternative under B(P)R regulations 30–32, involving an assessment/modification route, rather than permission to obstruct the window. No bedroom aisle, shelf depth or sofa-bed clearance is certified here as an HK statutory minimum. [BD APP-130, paragraphs 1–3](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP130.pdf).

Next deliverables are a measured 1:50 plan showing both furniture modes and all operating envelopes, window elevation/section, 1:5 mounting/joinery details, and separate fixed-storage and loose FF&E schedules. Keep product/model, size, sample, supplier, lead time and supply responsibility marked **to be confirmed** until the checks above are complete.
