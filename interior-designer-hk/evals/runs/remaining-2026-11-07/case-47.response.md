# Compliance gap memo — layout issue on hold

**Hong Kong residential flat | 7 November 2026 | Advisory review, not for construction**

I cannot issue the proposed two-bedroom layout as compliant. **Glass above the partition and a split air conditioner do not establish compliance for the windowless inner bedroom.**

Bedrooms are habitable rooms. Building (Planning) Regulations 30–31 require natural lighting and ventilation through qualifying windows; the ordinary prescriptive route includes glazing facing directly into external air. Borrowed light from the outer room is not equivalent to that provision. [Government explanation of regulation 30](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/201905/08/P2019050800337p.htm).

A conventional split AC principally recirculates and cools room air; it does not establish an outdoor-air supply or the required natural ventilation. Internal glazing may improve perceived brightness, but it does not demonstrate either statutory lighting or ventilation compliance.

| Gap | Status | Required resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Inner bedroom has no external window | Fails the ordinary window-based approach described above | Redesign to provide qualifying external windows, or obtain an Authorized Person’s substantiated alternative and the Building Authority’s acceptance before relying on it |
| Window dimensions, openable areas, external obstructions and room areas | Unknown | Measure and check each resulting habitable room separately |
| Existing approved layout, partition construction and escape arrangement | Unknown | Review approved records and survey before fixing doors or partitions |

BD’s **APP-130, paragraphs 1–3**, provides a performance-based route for natural lighting and ventilation, with justified assessment and modification of prescriptive requirements. It is not an automatic exemption for windowless bedrooms, and adding AC is not that assessment. [APP-130, December 2016 revision](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP130.pdf).

The viable redesign directions are:

- **Two bedrooms along the external façade**, only if the existing window arrangement can adequately serve each room and the remaining habitable accommodation also retains compliant provision.
- **Retain one compliant bedroom**, using the inner area for genuinely ancillary storage/dressing use subject to appropriate checks. Relabelling a sleeping room as a store does not solve the problem.
- **AP-led feasibility review** if two bedrooms remain essential and the façade cannot serve both. Do not build on an assumed exemption.

Please provide a dimensioned existing plan, window sizes/opening details and external views, ceiling heights, proposed occupancy, and available approved plans. These are needed to develop a reviewable 1:50 redesign. The works classification, structural/fire implications and estate requirements must also be checked; even exempt or minor works must satisfy applicable building requirements. [BD domestic alterations FAQ](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/faq/index_alteration_and_addition_works_in_domestic_premises.html).
