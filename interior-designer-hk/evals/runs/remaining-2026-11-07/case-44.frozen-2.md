| Certify this split-AC-only flat under the offices IAQ scheme | **Ineligible on the described basis.** The voluntary scheme covers offices/public places served by MVAC; split/window AC alone is excluded. An ordinary private flat also does not meet the stated use category. | Commission a residential IAQ assessment covering sources, ventilation, moisture/mould and representative measurements, reporting its scope without claiming this scheme’s certificate. Eligible premises with both MVAC and split/window units may participate; adding MVAC alone does not change residential-use eligibility. Certification of eligible premises requires an accredited Certificate Issuing Body, walkthrough, remediation, measurements/mould assessment and registration. [April 2026 IAQ Guide, §§2–9 and §5 footnote](https://www.iaq.gov.hk/common/form/Guide_on_IAQ_Certification_Scheme_eng.pdf). |
| Assume every project below 500 m² is energy-exempt | **Rejected: 500 m² is not a universal exemption.** | Screen statutory coverage and actual services work with a Registered Energy Assessor (REA), using the applicable BEC edition and dates. Do not issue an exemption from floor area alone. |

For the energy screen, distinguish the two major-retrofitting routes: area-based services additions/replacements in a unit or common area, including relevant phased work aggregated within 12 months; and qualifying main-component work to central building services, which is a separate route. An example is replacement of a complete central electrical circuit rated at least 400 A. Apply the detailed installation conditions in **Schedule 3 and BEC §10/Table 10.1**. Finishes-only work does not automatically trigger major-retrofitting duties. [EMSD major-retrofitting FAQ, Q1–4](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html).

The MEP scope record for REA review must identify building/use category; unit versus common-area or central services; installations retained, added or replaced; affected internal floor areas; equipment capacities and electrical ratings; all related phases within 12 months; and completion dates. The flat’s split AC description alone does not establish the building’s statutory coverage or the scope of other works. EMSD lists **BEC 2024** and directs users to Technical Circular 1/2024 for effective dates. Confirm the applicable version and transition before finalising specifications. [EMSD codes and technical guidelines](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_beeo_codtechguidelines.html).

The replacement deliverable is a credit-and-evidence register plus an energy-applicability assessment: source/version → applicable requirement → proposed measure → evidence → responsible assessor → unresolved action. Keep voluntary certification, statutory compliance and client contractual targets distinct. No BEAM score can be calculated without the selected credit set and project evidence.
