# Actual answer

**Hold the platform proposal.** Keeping the anchors untouched does not preserve the barrier’s effective height.

| Level check | Dimension |
|---|---:|
| Barrier top above original slab | 1,100 mm |
| Proposed finished platform above that same slab | 200 mm |
| Barrier height above the new step-on surface | **900 mm** |
| Shortfall against the HK 1,100 mm criterion | **200 mm** |

This calculation takes your 200 mm as the completed platform level above the original slab; confirm the surveyed datum and all finish layers. If 200 mm instead means an addition above an existing finish, the effective height may be lower still.

For an HK balcony, BD’s **APP-110, paragraphs 2–3**, gives a minimum barrier height of 1.1 m measured from the adjoining finished surface on which people can step. Its limited curb exceptions require specific geometry; a usable platform is not automatically an exempt curb. On the stated dimensions, an unchanged barrier beside a step-on platform fails that height criterion. [BD APP-110, January 2026 revision](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/signed/APP110se.pdf).

Under the skill’s compliance rule prioritising life safety, do not construct this arrangement pending redesign. The available remedial paths are:

- **Omit the platform:** verify the barrier height from the actual adjoining finished floor, since the slab measurement alone does not establish compliance.
- **Redesign its location/geometry:** an Authorized Person (AP) should confirm that it does not create an accessible step or climbing aid beside the barrier. Do not assume an arbitrary setback solves the issue.
- **Retain the raised surface only with a professionally designed barrier solution:** obtain AP/Registered Structural Engineer (RSE) assessment and the applicable approvals. A nominal 200 mm extension is not a complete design; no anchor or glazing specification can be issued from these dimensions.

Before release, provide a measured plan/section showing the slab, existing finish, proposed finished platform, barrier top, platform width/setback and any climbable features. The AP/RSE must review the existing barrier and its load path, new platform dead/imposed loads and balcony capacity. Coordinate waterproofing continuity/upstands, drainage falls, outlets and overflow access so the platform does not trap water or conceal maintenance points. Detail the platform edge and door threshold transitions to avoid trips and compromised access.

**Estate/management consent and the statutory approval route remain separate checks**; unchanged anchors or estate permission do not resolve the statutory review. Confirm the project jurisdiction, approved balcony records, barrier type and platform construction before a final project determination. This is an advisory hold, not professional certification.
