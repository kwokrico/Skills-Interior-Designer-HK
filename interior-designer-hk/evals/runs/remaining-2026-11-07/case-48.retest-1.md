# Actual answer

**No. You cannot treat the two phases as automatically below the major-retrofit threshold.** For the same series of works in the same unit, the relevant internal floor areas aggregate within 12 months. Your stated phases total **300 + 250 = 550 m² in eight months**, exceeding the 500 m² area threshold, provided these are separate qualifying internal floor areas. Splitting the project into phases does not give each phase a fresh exemption. [EMSD major-retrofitting FAQ, Q2](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html)

However, **meeting the area test does not alone establish that these lighting works are major retrofitting works**. The lighting-specific conditions must also be checked under BEC 2024 Table 10.1(a)(i):

| Check | Information needed |
|---|---|
| Luminaire scope | Confirm replacement of complete luminaires rather than lamps/tubes only. |
| Circuit wattage | Total circuit wattage of the additional/replacement luminaires: the table sets **at or exceeding 3 kW**. |
| Lighting power density applicability | Whether replacement/additional circuit wattage exceeds **50% of the original luminaires’ circuit wattage** in the area, or there were no existing luminaires. |
| Control applicability | Whether the existing lighting circuits are completely rewired, or the area had no existing luminaires; this engages the corresponding lighting-control provisions. |

These are the table’s separate applicability checks, not a requirement that every row must be satisfied simultaneously. [BEC 2024, §10 and Table 10.1, printed p.68](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/pee/BEC_2024_ENG.pdf)

Before committing either phase, give a **Registered Energy Assessor (REA)** the combined programme, marked-up internal-area plans, existing/proposed luminaire and circuit-wattage schedules, and wiring/control scope. Have the REA document applicability, the governing edition and any Form of Compliance requirements for the combined works. Coordinate this with the electrical designer so phase one preserves the required power-density and control strategy. Any additional central-services work needs its own screening; 500 m² is not a universal exemption. [EMSD FAQ, Q1 and Q8](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html)
