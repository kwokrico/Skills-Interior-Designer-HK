# Pre-concealment hold: do not cover the services yet

**No—phone photos alone do not make later drilling safe. Stop the proposed ceiling chasing and hold concealment until the routes, inspections and tests are resolved.**

| Issue | Required action before closure |
|---|---|
| Photographs | Keep wide views and close-ups with date, room/wall ID, orientation and dimensions from durable reference points. Mark routes, crossings and changes on a coordinated drawing; update after rectification and retain with as-built/O&M records. |
| Later drilling | Photos are supporting records, not clearance to drill. A competent person must verify current concealed services and substrate at the actual fixing position, using appropriate detection and safe working methods. Resolve uncertainty before drilling; use a different fixing location/method where necessary. |
| “Whole-flat concealed wiring” | Clarify the route and exclusions on a drawing. Conduits surface-mounted within a false-ceiling void are concealed from the occupied room; that does **not** mean conduits must be chased into the original structural ceiling. |
| Original ceiling chasing | Do not authorize chasing beams or slabs on the strength of a quotation. Establish whether the affected layer is finish or structural concrete. Where structure would be affected, obtain the appropriate building/structural professional assessment and required approvals first. Consider properly supported routes within a false ceiling, a service bulkhead or surface containment. |
| Water-pressure test (谷磅) | Arrange testing before concealment. A missing quotation line is not permission to omit required testing. Agree the applicable pipe-system method and acceptance criteria with the responsible plumbing professional; record the test and rectify/retest failures before closure. |

The immediate gate is **after service installation and before rendering, tiling or ceiling closure**. Walk the routes and point positions with the responsible trades against the coordinated drawings; check support, protection, interfaces and maintenance access. Owner photographs or a visual walk-through do not certify electrical or plumbing work. This follows the HKEDCA concealed-services gate summarized in the skill’s §1.10 and the [residential stage-inspection reference](<C:/Users/Rico/PycharmProjects/Skills-Interior Designer-HK/interior-designer-hk/subskills/interior-site-supervision/references/residential-stage-inspections.md>).

For a Hong Kong project, HKRG §裝修需知/1.4, general item 9, expressly calls for the contractor to provide a water-pressure test after pipe installation. Section 1.13, item 2, describes lighting conduits above a false ceiling as surface-run within that void; §1.4’s additional-charge list separately addresses concealed conduit work above false ceilings. These are industry/contract guidance, **not statutory authorization to cut structural concrete**. Check whether HKRG is incorporated in your signed contract and settle any genuine cost/time issue in writing without releasing untested work. [HKRG trade extract](<C:/Users/Rico/PycharmProjects/Skills-Interior Designer-HK/interior-designer-hk/subskills/interior-site-supervision/references/hkrg-renovation-essentials-trades.md>)

Have the registered electrical contractor confirm the actual compliant wiring system, its protection/support and inspection/testing requirements. Do not assume every cable type uses identical conduit, or that wiring needs no protection because it is hidden. Electrical testing and completion evidence remain separate from the water test. EMSD provides an [inspection/test checklist](https://bestpractice.emsd.gov.hk/en/electrical-installations/useful-forms) and lists the [applicable Wiring Regulations Codes of Practice and transition arrangements](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/en/electricity_safety/publications/codes_of_practice/index.html).

**Suggested hold-point wording for the appointed contract administrator to issue:**

> Hold closure of the affected service zones and suspend proposed chasing of the original ceiling. Submit coordinated routes distinguishing structural concrete, finishes and false-ceiling voids; resolve structural/approval concerns; complete applicable concealed-work inspections and water-pressure testing; rectify and retest failures; provide updated dimensioned photographs, drawings and test records. Release each zone only after documented acceptance by the responsible appointed parties. Confirm any cost/programme changes under the contract.

The water-test record should identify the system and tested extent, approved method, pressure and duration, gauge, date, observations, witness and retest result. **No numerical pressure or duration can be prescribed from the information supplied.** The ceiling construction, actual wiring/pipe systems, contract and approval conditions remain unverified. The skill’s [compliance rules](<C:/Users/Rico/PycharmProjects/Skills-Interior Designer-HK/interior-designer-hk/references/compliance.md>) prohibit authorizing unauthorized demolition or giving licensed sign-off; the hold is pending competent review, not a design approval.
