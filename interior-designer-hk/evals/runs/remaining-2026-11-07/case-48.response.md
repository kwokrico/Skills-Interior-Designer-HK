# Response

**No. The two phases cannot automatically be treated as below the major-retrofit threshold.** For the same series of works in one prescribed office unit, the relevant works areas aggregate within 12 months. On the stated figures, **300 + 250 = 550 m²**, with the second phase eight months later: the 500 m² area criterion is met, subject to confirming these are the relevant internal works areas without overlap. Splitting the project into phases or contracts does not reset that test. [EMSD major-retrofitting FAQ, questions 2 and 9](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html).

**Area alone does not settle the final classification.** Under BEC 2024 §10, Table 10.1(a)(i), check:

| Check | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Total circuit wattage of added/replacement luminaires **at least 3 kW** | Installation-specific threshold; obtain the actual circuit-wattage schedule. |
| No existing luminaires, or replacement/additional circuit wattage **more than 50% of the original luminaires' circuit wattage** in the area | Lighting-power-density requirements under §5.4 apply when the other applicability conditions are met. |
| No existing luminaires, or **complete rewiring of existing lighting circuits** in the area | Check lighting control points and automatic lighting controls under §§5.5–5.6. |

These are luminaire and circuit-wattage tests, not simply fixture-count percentages or a lux check. [BEC 2024, Table 10.1, printed p.68](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/pee/BEC_2024_ENG.pdf).

Before proceeding on an exemption basis, have a **Registered Energy Assessor (REA)** review the two-phase programme, marked internal works areas, existing/new luminaire circuit wattages, replacement proportion and rewiring scope together. Confirm the applicable code edition and any exclusions. If classified as major retrofitting works, design the affected installations for the applicable BEC requirements and arrange the REA's Form of Compliance within two months after completion of the works. [EMSD TG-BEC 2024, §10.1.7 and phased-work examples](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/pee/TG-BEC_2024.pdf).

The decision supported now is **“area trigger met; lighting applicability assessment required”**, rather than an automatic exemption for either phase.
