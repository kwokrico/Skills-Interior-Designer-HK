## 4. 總則 — soft general notes

Use the following as a design coordination checklist. They are proposed practice notes, not additional statutory rules or new compliance hard stops.

- [ ] Agree physical samples, colour/texture, edges and hardware, with datasheets or a mock-up where useful, before ordering and installation. Record the approved code and date.
- [ ] Remeasure on site and reconcile drawings before tender quantities and fabrication are fixed. State measured quantities, agreed waste allowance and purchase quantities separately.
- [ ] For low-emission boards, request the actual product test report and record standard, edition, method and specimen scope. Do not treat E1, E0, ENF or other national classes as automatically equivalent.
- [ ] Prefer low-VOC paint, adhesive and sealant systems; request VOC information and relevant safety data sheets. Low odour alone is not performance evidence.
- [ ] Use suitable moisture-resistant joinery in wet-risk locations; detail sealed cut-outs/edges, penetrations and a sink-base protection tray where appropriate. Moisture-resistant board does not replace waterproofing.
- [ ] Review floor slip evidence for actual use, including barefoot showers; an R rating alone is insufficient. Coordinate falls, drainage, tile modules and cleaning needs.
- [ ] Confirm a compatible waterproofing/adhesive/grout system, coverage details, curing, testing and protection before tiling; record the agreed test method and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Confirm safe glazing specification and fixings with the supplier for each use. Review edge treatment, impact risk and opening clearance; do not use cabinet glass as structural support.
- [ ] Coordinate metal colours across handles, taps, trims and shower hardware, and check corrosion resistance in wet zones.
- [ ] Check floor build-ups and thresholds before manufacture. Refer level differences over 3 mm for a coordinated transition detail; this is the skill's design review trigger, not a statutory accessibility limit.
- [ ] Review lighting colour together with the finish samples; 3000 K and CRI ≥90 are proposed design targets. Verify wet-location suitability, controls, cut-outs and accessible drivers for the selected fittings.
- [ ] Flag kitchen/bathroom, partition, fire-separation or building-services changes for the relevant estate/authority and professional review. A finish approval does not approve those alterations.
- [ ] Confirm stock, batch/shade, fabrication lead time and delivery constraints before selection is locked. Track bespoke joinery, worktops, glazing and appliances against the programme; no live lead times are assumed here.

**To complete Rev 01:** obtain the measured plan and retention list; owner selections and appliance models; agreed supplier/installer responsibilities; itemised quantities; coordinated drawings; and sample/technical approvals. HKEDCA/HKRG inform industry practice and coordination, not statutory certification.
