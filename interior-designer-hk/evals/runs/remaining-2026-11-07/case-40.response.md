# Bedroom furnishing brief — provisional, not for fabrication

**Use inventory-led storage, a coordinated blackout treatment and a reserved sofa-bed operating zone. Do not label the book’s dimensions as Hong Kong legal minima.** Ching and Binggeli’s examples are design guidance; actual users, product drawings and applicable local requirements govern. The skill’s compliance rule 2 expressly prohibits presenting global defaults as statutory fact.

Final shelf depths, curtain mounting and furniture clearances remain on hold until there is a measured room/window survey and a sofa-bed size envelope. This does not prevent setting the following design brief.

| Element | Proposed design direction | Evidence needed before release |
|---|---|---|
| Storage | Do not specify deep shelves everywhere. Inventory each category by quantity, item dimensions, access frequency, visibility and expected growth. Separate display and daily-use storage from bulky/seasonal storage. Use shallow adjustable shelves where they suit the contents; reserve deeper bays for measured bulky items. Drawers or pull-outs can improve retrieval from deep storage but need their own opening space. | Item inventory; user reach needs; usable internal dimensions; shelf spans/loads and support details; door/drawer opening envelopes and cleaning access. |
| Future sofa bed | Keep the conversion zone free of fixed joinery. Compare movable/modular pieces with built-ins so the room can change later. Plan both sofa and fully deployed sleeping arrangements, including the conversion movement, bedding, user access and a usable route to the bedroom door. | Manufacturer’s closed/open dimensions and wall offsets, operating instructions, user trial, delivery package dimensions and lift/door/turning route. If no model is chosen, agree a provisional maximum envelope and recheck before purchase. |
| Inward-opening window | Plot the full sash and projecting handle sweep in plan and section. Keep shelves, the open sofa bed, curtain fabric and stacked treatment out of that operating zone. Preserve access for opening, ventilation and cleaning. | Measured sash geometry, reveal depth, sill/head heights, handle projection, available wall/ceiling mounting substrate and side stacking space. |
| Blackout curtains | Fabric curtains remain an option if a coordinated track and stack-back arrangement clears the inward-opening sash. Specify the required room-darkening result, not just “blackout fabric”: check head, side, centre-overlap and bottom light leakage. Assess returns/overlaps and head treatment without obstructing window operation. | Day/night privacy, view, glare and darkness requirements; supplier’s track/fabric assembly drawing; sample and on-site light-leakage check; cleaning/replacement access and safe controls. |

If curtain stacking cannot clear the window, compare a suitably positioned shade or blind with the same swing check. A headbox, raised fabric bundle or side channel can also obstruct an inward-opening sash, so a blind is not automatically the solution. Confirm control reach and the selected system’s child-safety/installation requirements; coordinate power and maintenance access if motorised. Do not assume blackout cloth establishes thermal, acoustic or fire performance.

The furnishing principles above follow *Interior Design Illustrated*, third edition: furniture grouping pp. 319–321, storage pp. 340–345, and window treatments pp. 346–349, as summarised in the project’s furnishings reference.

**HK compliance boundary:** preserve the approved daylight and ventilation provision when positioning tall storage and window coverings. BD’s APP-130 addresses a performance-based alternative under Building (Planning) Regulations 30–32; it is not permission to obstruct an opening or substitute textbook dimensions for local requirements. Any proposal affecting that provision needs the applicable approval basis checked by the responsible professional. [BD APP-130, paragraphs 1–3](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP130.pdf).

Provide a dimensioned room sketch with door/window swings, the storage inventory, intended users and any mobility needs, and the likely sofa-bed model or desired sleeping size. The next deliverable is a 1:50 test-fit showing daytime and sleeping layouts, followed by 1:5 window/joinery interface details once the products and survey are confirmed.
