| Issue | Required action before closure |
|---|---|
| Phone photos | While routes remain exposed, take wide and close views labelled with room/wall, date and orientation. Include measured offsets from permanent reference points, crossings, changes of direction and a marked route drawing. Update these after rectification and retain them with the as-built records. Before any later drilling, a competent installer must verify current services and substrate using suitable detection and safe working methods; photos cannot establish a safe fixing location or depth. |
| “Whole-flat concealed wiring” | Clarify the routes and construction in writing. Conduits exposed **within a false-ceiling void** are different from conduits **chased into the original slab**. The quotation does not authorize structural cutting. Stop the proposed slab/beam chasing pending identification of the substrate, professional structural assessment and applicable permissions. A coordinated route within a false ceiling/bulkhead, or suitable surface containment, may avoid structural chasing. Confirm height, support, maintenance access and fire-service interfaces before selecting it. |
| Wiring protection | Have the registered electrical contractor confirm the actual compliant cable/containment system, supports, protection and inspection/testing. Do not translate “concealed” into unprotected wiring, or prescribe identical conduits for every cable type. EMSD provides inspection and test checklists, including false-ceiling wiring protection checks. [EMSD sample forms and checklists](https://bestpractice.emsd.gov.hk/en/electrical-installations/useful-forms). |
| Water-pressure test omitted from quotation | Keep the pipework exposed and arrange the required pressure test. HKRG §裝修需知/1.4, general item 9, calls for the contractor to provide the test after pipe installation; HKEDCA water/electrical practice places testing before concealment. These are industry guides, whose contractual application must be checked—not a claim that every guide provision is law. Resolve scope and payment separately; omission is not technical permission to omit testing. |

Have the responsible plumbing trade establish the applicable test method and acceptance criteria from the actual pipe system, specification and relevant authority requirements. Record **system and tested extent, method, pressure, duration, gauge identification, observations, witness, result and any rectification/retest**. No universal pressure or duration can be specified from the information provided. A water-pressure test does not replace electrical inspection/testing or a wet-area waterproofing test.

Before cover-up, the contractor and responsible inspector should reconcile installed routes with the coordinated drawings, inspect supports and interfaces, preserve necessary valve/junction access, close defects and record the release of each inspected area. Keep electrical completion evidence separate from an owner's visual inspection. The registered contractor must confirm the applicable [EMSD Wiring Regulations Code of Practice](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/en/electricity_safety/publications/codes_of_practice/index.html).

Submit the revised route drawing distinguishing false-ceiling containment from any proposed structural chasing; confirm the wiring system, pre-cover inspection and test plan; complete required water-pressure testing and relevant electrical inspection/testing, rectify and retest defects, and provide measured route records before requesting release. Reconcile any disputed cost/time in writing without waiving the inspection hold.”
