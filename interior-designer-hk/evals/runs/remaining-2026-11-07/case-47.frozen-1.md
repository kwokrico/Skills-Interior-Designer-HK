# Compliance gap memo — two-bedroom subdivision

**Status: HOLD — layout not issued for construction.**  
**Jurisdiction:** Hong Kong · **Review date:** 7 November 2026  
**Known brief:** Divide an existing living room into two bedrooms; inner bedroom has no external window. No measured plan or approved building records supplied.

Glass above the partition and a split air conditioner do **not** establish bedroom compliance. Borrowed light is different from the required natural-light provision, and a typical split AC recirculates indoor air for cooling; it does not demonstrate an outdoor-air ventilation supply.

The ordinary B(P)R regulations 30–31 route requires habitable rooms to have prescribed windows for natural lighting and ventilation, including qualifying external exposure. Internal partition glazing does not establish that provision for the inner bedroom. [Government explanation of regulations 30–31](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/201607/13/P2016071301395p.htm).

An alternative assessment is possible only through the applicable statutory process. **APP-130 is not an automatic permission for a windowless bedroom:** paragraphs 1–3 concern natural lighting and natural ventilation, supported by substantiated assessment and an application to modify the prescriptive requirements. An Authorized Person (AP) must establish the appropriate route and demonstrate performance to the Building Authority. Its office-conversion provisions cannot simply be applied to this domestic bedroom. [BD APP-130, December 2016 revision](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP130.pdf).

| Gap | Status / severity | Required resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Inner bedroom natural light and ventilation | Not demonstrated; high | Reconfigure to provide qualifying external windows, or obtain AP assessment and any required Building Authority approval before relying on an alternative. |
| Window allocation after subdivision | Unknown; high | Survey each proposed room's floor area, glazing and effective openable area, window-head level, external obstructions and approved window arrangements. Check both rooms and any remaining habitable space. |
| Escape and fire protection | Unknown; high | Check each room's access to the flat exit, doors and swings, any route through another bedroom, kitchen fire separation, and detector/sprinkler coverage where present. Do not compromise existing protection. |
| Cooling, ventilation and electrical services | Uncoordinated | Separately establish outdoor-air strategy, cooling loads, AC locations, condensate drainage, refrigerant routes, electrical supply and maintenance access. A larger AC does not remedy the daylight gap. |
| Lighting | No design evidence | Once a lawful room arrangement is established, develop ambient/task lighting and glare control, coordinated with partition/ceiling heights and accessible drivers. Artificial lux levels are not proof of statutory daylight compliance. |
| Works/approval classification | Unknown; high | Review approved records and partition construction with the appropriate building professional; determine applicable minor-works or approval requirements and separate estate/DMC restrictions. |

BD distinguishes exempt works, minor works and works requiring approval; even exempt work must comply with relevant building regulations. Management consent does not replace statutory compliance. [BD domestic alteration FAQ](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/faq/index_alteration_and_addition_works_in_domestic_premises.html).

**Remediated directions for feasibility review:**

1. Place both bedrooms along a suitable external window frontage, if its dimensions and openings can independently serve both rooms. Confirm by measurement and statutory review before fixing the partition.
2. Retain one compliant bedroom and use the inner area only for genuinely non-habitable ancillary storage, subject to review; do not label it a store and use it for sleeping. Preserve the required provision to the remaining living accommodation.
3. If two bedrooms cannot fit with adequate windows and escape arrangements, retain the existing room configuration or investigate a different whole-flat arrangement with the AP. Do not construct the proposed inner bedroom pending an unapproved exception.

**Release information required:** dimensioned existing/approved plans; window sizes, opening types and external context; room and ceiling heights; proposed occupancy and furniture requirements; door/exit and kitchen arrangement; structural-wall identification; and existing services/fire-system information. The next deliverable can then be a **1:50 feasibility plan and section marked for review**, with room-by-room light/ventilation evidence and unresolved approval items clearly identified.
