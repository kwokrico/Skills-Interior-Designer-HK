No. You cannot treat the two phases as automatically below the major-retrofit threshold.

For the same series of works in the same prescribed office unit, the areas must be assessed together within the 12-month window. On the stated figures, **300 + 250 = 550 m² over eight months**, so the area threshold is reached if these are distinct qualifying internal floor areas. Confirm the measured boundaries and any overlap; do not assess each phase in isolation. [EMSD major-retrofitting FAQ, question 2](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html).

**That establishes the area trigger, not the entire lighting applicability test.** BEC 2024 Table 10.1(a)(i) also requires checking:

| Check | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Replacement/additional luminaires have total circuit wattage **at least 3 kW** | Luminaire and controlgear schedules, circuit wattages across the works |
| Lighting power density applicability | Whether replacement/additional circuit wattage exceeds 50% of original luminaire circuit wattage in the area, or there were no existing luminaires |
| Lighting control applicability | Whether the area had no existing luminaires or existing lighting circuits are completely rewired |

The corresponding requirements are lighting power density (§5.4), lighting control points (§5.5), and automatic lighting control (§5.6), according to the applicable row. The code says **at or exceeding 3 kW**; FAQ question 7 uses “exceeding,” so use the code's inclusive boundary. [BEC 2024, §10, Table 10.1, printed p.68](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/pee/BEC_2024_ENG.pdf).

Before proceeding on an exemption basis, have a **Registered Energy Assessor (REA)** review both phases together: measured plans, programme/completion dates, existing and proposed lighting wattages, retained fittings, wiring/control scope, and any other services replaced under the project. Confirm the applicable BEC edition and Form of Compliance route. Any central-services component works need a separate screen; 500 m² is not a universal exemption. [EMSD FAQ, questions 1 and 8](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_faq_mrworks_a.html); [EMSD code index](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/beeo/en/mibec_beeo_codtechguidelines.html).

Decision: **reject the automatic below-threshold classification; final applicability remains pending the lighting scope and REA assessment.**
