## What is covered, and what still needs checking

| Sheet | Coverage established by your description | Checks before marking complete |
|---|---|---|
| P0 — existing plan | Existing-condition baseline present | Verify measured dimensions, openings, levels and retained services; distinguish retained, removed and new work through a demolition/retention plan or clear coordinated annotations. P0 does not replace a site survey. |
| P1 — design plan | Proposed layout and L/S/K/B joinery tags present | Check dimensions, elevation bubbles and tag continuity: L living/dining, S bedroom/study, K kitchen, B bathroom. Link each item to its elevation, specification and pricing line; mark owner-supplied items 自購. |
| P2 — floor/sill material plan | Finish-location coverage present | Confirm every hatch has a code and every code maps to the consolidated material schedule. Show skirting extent and wet-area exceptions, sill/threshold materials, finished levels and transition references. |
| P3 — ceiling/lighting plan | Ceiling and lighting intent present | Verify symbol legend, fixture references, finished clear heights (+H), bulkheads/AC niches, exhaust, access panels and switching intent. Drawing presence does not establish service coordination. |
| P4–P7 — room elevations | Room elevation coverage present | Verify all required walls are covered; continuous elevation numbering matches P1 bubbles; materials and L/S/K/B tags agree. Check dimensions, levels and intentional equal bays. At 1:50 these communicate arrangement, but cannot resolve all bespoke construction and pricing details. |

Across the set, check sheet titles/numbers, date, revision, units, issue purpose and A4 print legibility. State site-verification and discrepancy-reporting duties. Revision-match the complete issue; do not rely on measuring scaled prints to resolve missing dimensions.

| T07 | **BOQ/pricing schedule and contract attachments:** consistent descriptions, units, quantities or stated measurement basis, scope boundaries, preliminaries, exclusions and defined allowances. Add the adapted HKRG quotation attachment if that form is adopted. | Drawings alone do not establish comparable bids or the commercial scope. Designer/QS/contract administrator. |

For **自購**, exclude the owner's purchase cost from contractor supply pricing, but separately define any contractor delivery handling, storage, installation, connection and testing obligations. Record item/model confirmation and delivery dates on an owner-supply schedule; avoid both omissions and double counting.

The full invitation also needs tender instructions/deadline, form of tender, programme, conditions and appendices, specification, document register and tender summary. Define protection, estate constraints, insurance responsibilities, acceptance/handover scope, variations and defects arrangements. Align BOQ measurement with the chosen HKEDCA basis and contract/acceptance provisions with HKRG where adopted; these guides are industry practice, not statutory law. Adapt model contract wording with legal review and use the actual contract's document precedence.

## Release gate

1. Assign each gap an owner, due date and drawing/schedule/BOQ reference.
2. Resolve information critical to safety, buildability and firm pricing. For genuinely unresolved commercial selections or survey unknowns, state a common scope and explicit allowance/provisional sum with valuation rules; do not let bidders silently assume different solutions.
3. Cross-check **P1 tag → elevation/detail → material/fixture schedule → BOQ**, and **P3 ceiling/lighting → services → joinery**.
4. Reissue a revision-matched package and distribute any tender clarification/addendum equally to all bidders.

Until those gates close, the set can support a clearly labelled preliminary budget exercise, but should not be represented as ready for comparable firm-price tenders. No specific statutory violation can be concluded from the sheet list alone.
