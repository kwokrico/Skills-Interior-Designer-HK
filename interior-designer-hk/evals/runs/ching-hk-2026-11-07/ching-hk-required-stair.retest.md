For the stated use classification **5a**, the BD *Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 (2024 Edition)* requires the following corrections:

| Item | Proposed | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| Flight form | Winder | Straight flights without winders — B14.2 and B23.1(a) |
| Tread | 225 mm | At least **280 mm**, clear of nosings — B14.2(a), B23.1(e) |
| Riser | 175 mm | At most **150 mm** — B14.2(a), B23.1(e) |

Also apply the 5a-specific flight provisions: **3–16 steps per flight**, no more than two successive flights without a turn, and a turn between successive flights if either exceeds 12 steps. Check landing dimensions and door swings, clear headroom of at least 2000 mm, required width, returning handrails and guarded recesses. See B14.3–B14.4 and B23.1–B23.3. [BD Fire Safety Code, printed pp.44 and 55](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/fs_code2011.pdf).

Replace the winder with a landing arrangement and revise the stair section to those dimensions.
