# HKEDCA — Standard Interior Works Contract (室內工程標準合同)

Source: `HKEDCA §室內工程標準合同`, PDF ~p.178–199. Template only — legal review required per project.

## Typical contract structure (outline)

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Parties | Owner and contractor identities |
| Scope of works | Trade list aligned to 清拆→交收 sequence |
| Contract sum | Fixed or re-measurement basis |
| Programme | Commencement, sectional completion, liquidated damages if any |
| Payment | Stages tied to milestones; retention |
| Variations | Instruction method, pricing basis |
| Quality | Reference to guide/workmanship; samples and mock-ups |
| Insurance | Contractor all-risks / third party as required |
| Defects liability | Period and remedy mechanism |
| Termination | Default and convenience clauses |
| Dispute resolution | Arbitration or courts per agreement |

## Tender QA — contract completeness checks

| Risk | Check |
|---|---|
| Missing protection obligations | Include 清拆 Ch.2 protection matrix by reference |
| Unclear measurement rules | Attach cross-trade 工程計算規則 summary |
| No handover standard | Reference 交收 acceptance methods |
| Gas works | Explicitly excluded — registered gas contractor only |
| Estate rules | Management office permit conditions attached |
| Scaffold/safety | LD/FSD compliance; appendix safety where exterior works |

## Alignment with BOQ

- Each BOQ section should map to contract scope clause.
- Provisional sums for unknown demolition, MEP congestion, and stone lead times.
- Clarify who supplies owner-selected materials (鋁窗, 磁磚, 冷氣).

## When to escalate

- Non-standard liability caps, unlimited variation, or absent DLP → flag for legal review before tender issue.
