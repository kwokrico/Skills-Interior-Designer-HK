# Leak-path field notes

> Source: 工程師裝修學堂 transcripts extracted 2026-11-01 (`Reference/renovationeer_extract`). Field commentary, not HKEDCA, HKRG, or statute.
> On conflict: statute, AP/RSE, manufacturer method statement, then the signed contract.
> Do not copy spoken numbers the transcript flags as Whisper errors.

Stack depths, upstand dimensions, and flood-test gates stay in `interior-thickness-build-up` and `HKRG §裝修需知/1.7`. This file is the failure path, not a second millimetre table.

## What a “coat” means

- A cementitious system is “two coats, about 1.5 mm” **only when that manufacturer’s current sheet says so**. More coats add weight and can debond if the interface is wrong.
- Compare systems by **coverage consumption**, not the word “coat”. One kit that “finished” a large toilet is a question, not a record.
- A quoted “third” or “fourth” coat is often render with a latex additive, or a bonding agent. Latex, bonding agent, and modified render are **not** membranes. They only make joints slightly less leaky.
- False waterproofing tells: 「牛奶水」 used as waterproofing, and a film wiped on a plastic bag passed off as a membrane.
- BD PNAP APP-105 is design guidance for water seepage. It is **not** a product approval. Do not treat coat count as compliance.
- A flood test (pond water and watch the level) does not replace the details below.

## Details to keep

- Form the bathroom **door upstand** before tiling, so splash cannot travel out on the membrane under the floor finish and show at the threshold.
- Round internal corners (cove / 圓角), or use the system’s prefabricated corner and say so in the price. A sharp arris leaves a gap the brush misses.
- On hollow or cracked walls, mesh first, then render. Prefer the membrane maker’s fibre mesh. Chicken wire is steel.
- Do the door detail before the floor is tiled. Trades often finish walls and leave the floor.

## Leak catalogue (diagnostic only)

Separate internal sources (bath, shower, membrane, supply, waste, WC, drain) from external (window reveal, external wall, AC, pipe through the wall). Condensation and 回南天 look wet and are not a leak. A material crack is one line; a leak blister is a loose area.

| Where | What to check | What not to specify |
|---|---|---|
| Bath | Joint at the rim, joint between tub and tile, waste and trap. Old private blocks sometimes have a membrane and weep under the tub; many new buildings do not. Marble upstands are not waterproof. Water standing in the dry gully means the layer above has already failed | A surface silicone as the permanent repair |
| Shower | Membrane under the tray, and the waste connected at the low point. A plinth built up to suit a high waste leaves a void: water runs between waste and membrane | Pouring membrane in from the top. That is symptomatic |
| Window / external wall | Hollow render (tap test), perished rebate seal, a reveal that ponds. Aluminium sections are hollow, so water can travel inside the frame. Window-edge ownership is often split with the manager | DIY coating or fresh silicone as the repair. External walls are often common parts |
| AC | A blocked or split condensate joint. Concealed pipework may need the outdoor unit opened | Treating the drip as a bathroom membrane failure |
| Door / living-room side | Membrane stops at the floor and there is no upstand, so bathroom water travels under the finish. It can also be the neighbour | “Add another coat” on the dry side |

Salt in a bathroom can be the upstairs salt-water WC flush, not a fresh-water leak.

A moisture meter needs a **control wall** that almost cannot leak. One source calls a reading above about 70% high and 30–40% possibly ambient. The Joint Office path has used a different trigger (about 35% on its own instrument). **Do not merge those numbers.** Different instruments and protocols.

Infrared and dye show where water is, or whether a suspected waste is connected. They do not name the source. A shower floor that is supposed to be wet cannot be read as a leak.

## Negative side and upstairs

- A negative-side coating is a remedy when the wet face cannot be reached (basement, stair against a neighbour, failed external concrete). It does not replace repair at the source. Specify from the current method statement only — the source disagrees with itself on thickness and cure, so those figures stay out.
- Red lead plus site-mixed sand-cement is not a concrete repair. Match any repair mortar to the parent concrete; that match is an AP/RSE decision, not a brand name from this file. Spalling sequence and submissions: `structural-and-hos-alerts.md`.
- Waterproofing done only in the flat below is usually symptomatic. Designer path: tell the manager, then the Joint Office (聯辦處), then an AP, surveyor, or engineer report if access or proof is refused. Do not write court procedure, tribunal limits, or fine amounts. A 2026 private-building pilot exists; days, fees, and fines must be read from the current notice — see the statutory alerts file.
