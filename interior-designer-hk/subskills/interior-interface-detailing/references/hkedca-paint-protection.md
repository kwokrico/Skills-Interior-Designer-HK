# HKEDCA — Paint Works & Interface Protection (油漆工程)

Source: `HKEDCA §油漆/*`, PDF ~p.110–118.

## Paint structure (typical residential)

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| Primer / sealer | Substrate sealing |
| Undercoat | Build and adhesion |
| Topcoat | Finish colour and durability |

## Pre-paint requirements

- Plaster dry; cracks repaired; moisture content acceptable.
- Other trades' protection complete (windows, pipes, openings sealed).
- MEP faceplates: coordinate install timing — often after paint in residential.

## Application rules

- Ventilation maintained; **"油漆未乾"** signage on fresh work.
- Canvas on finished floors if painting in occupied areas.
- **Close windows before rain and end of shift** to prevent wash-off and humidity defects.
- Uniform film thickness; sand between coats where specified.

## Interface with other finishes

| Interface | Detail |
|---|---|
| Timber skirting | Scribe or shadow gap; paint seal to wet zones per spec |
| Tiles | Paint stop at tile edge or silicone joint — no free-hand overlap |
| Joinery | Factory finish vs site paint — define touch-up boundary |
| Ceiling | Coordinate with false ceiling install sequence |

## Measurement (工程計算規則)

- Wall/ceiling paint: m² by surface type (plain, textured).
- State whether doors, frames, and joinery included or scheduled separately.

## Handover

- No paint streaks on switches, glass, or tiles (交收 general cleaning).
- Nail-scrape adhesion check per acceptance method.
