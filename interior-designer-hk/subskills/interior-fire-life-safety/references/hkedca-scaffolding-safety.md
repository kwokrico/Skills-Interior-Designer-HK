# HKEDCA — Scaffolding & Working at Height (安全建議)

Source: `HKEDCA §附錄：安全建議`, PDF ~p.200–230. Align with latest **Labour Department** and **Buildings Department** publications.

## General suspended bamboo scaffold (一般懸空式竹棚架)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | By professional engineer |
| Erection/alteration/dismantling | Trained workers under direct supervision of competent person (not performing scaffold work themselves) |
| Sequence | Erect load-bearing members first; dismantle non-load-bearing first; check ties before dismantling |
| Loading | Minimize personnel and material on platform |
| Platforms | Work platform, guardrails, toe boards, safe access |
| Exclusion zone | Barricade below to prevent falling objects |
| Inspection | Before first use; every 14 days; after major alteration, partial dismantle, or adverse weather — competent person sign Form 5 |
| Weather | Stop work on tropical cycline / strong monsoon signals; secure scaffold and remove nets/material |
| Dismantle | Promptly after works to reduce unmaintained failure risk |

## Safety harness and anchorage (安全吊帶)

- Full-body harness + suspension rope / independent lifeline / fall arrest connectors for scaffold work.
- Anchorage ≥ **6 kN**; inspected/tested by structural/mechanical engineer or ACCP-qualified person for anchor bolts / cast-in anchors.
- Mobile temporary anchor devices (BS EN 795:2012 or equivalent) if fixed anchors infeasible — supervised by DOCC-trained person.
- **Do not** anchor to window frames, rails, temporary props, water/gas pipes, or scaffold members not designed for fall arrest.
- Lifeline only for fall protection — one person per lifeline; harness inspection every 12 months (or before use if unused 6 months).

## Estate coordination

- Owner/management should facilitate anchor installation on common/external areas where feasible for worker safety.

## Special scaffold types (特殊懸空式竹棚架)

- For facades with deep window projections, limited concrete for ties, etc.
- PolyU structural studies referenced in guide — **schematic only**; professional engineer design mandatory.
- Typical load limit example: **3 kN/m²** for two 90 kg workers doing heavy facade work (e.g. AC replacement) — verify against current engineer design.

## Interface with interior fire-life-safety

| Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Sprinkler/defector protection during interior works | Use guards/caps per site protection matrix |
| Fire alarm isolate | 掛牌 before hot works or dusty detector-triggering work |
| Means of escape | Never block during scaffold or material storage |

## Site supervision chain

Scaffold non-compliance → stop work → `interior-site-supervision` SI → re-inspection before resume.
