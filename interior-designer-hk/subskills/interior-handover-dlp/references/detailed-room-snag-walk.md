# Detailed room snag walk

Field evidence: HOMIC DÉCOR `7LoRtDN7kBw`, Summary / Transcript / Items. [Archived source and adoption record](../../../references/source-register.md#homic-décor-review-2026-11-06). Use with [HKRG self-inspection](hkrg-self-inspection-handover.md), not as replacement acceptance tolerances.

Inspect by room and trade, opening every accessible cupboard rather than relying on the overall appearance. Use the [snag log](../../../references/templates/snag-defect-log.md) for location, photograph, defect, agreed remedy, responsible party, due date and verified closure.

| Check | Additional inspection detail |
|---|---|
| Electrical and appliances | Cross-check the approved point schedule, including sockets/switches concealed inside cabinets. Confirm accessible controls, installed faceplates and normal light/AC operation. A phone charging proves limited function only; it cannot verify earthing, polarity, protective devices or installation safety. Do not open electrical equipment as an owner inspection. |
| Joinery | Check a bank of doors for aligned faces and consistent top/bottom reveals. Fully operate tall doors and drawers for binding, rubbing and collisions; test agreed bumpers/soft-close fittings. Inspect curved edge banding for lifting, including inconspicuous returns. |
| Finishing joints | Inspect agreed sealant/caulk details at cabinet sides, skirtings and returns for omission, roughness and poor adhesion. Do not infer that every gap must be sealed: preserve ventilation, drainage, movement and equipment access. Electrical enclosure details belong to the registered electrical contractor, not indiscriminate caulking. |
| Tiles | Check all four corners and adjacent tile junctions for lippage, damage and joint variation using the agreed inspection method. Appearance or touch alone cannot establish whether a defect comes from tile warpage, substrate or installation. |
| Paint | Inspect long exposed walls, pinholes, scratches, cracks and interfaces under agreed viewing conditions. Record defects immediately, even when cosmetic repair is deferred. |

Agree the sample, viewing conditions and workmanship standard before pricing/installation. The speaker's personal acceptance of one or two uneven joints and references to a “microscope” are not contractual tolerances or reasons to waive HKRG criteria where adopted.

The suggested two-to-three-month paint return is an optional planned visit for confirmed minor drying-shrinkage defects. Agree it in writing within the contract/DLP notice periods, retaining the initial snag record and rights. Do not defer leakage, electrical defects, progressive/wide cracks or suspected structural movement; investigate and escalate promptly instead of painting over them.

Electrical verification: [EMSD electrical safety checklist](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_442/Electrical_Safety_Checklist.pdf), checked 2026-11-06: use a registered electrical contractor and obtain inspection/testing and the applicable work completion certificate before energising for use. Functional owner checks do not replace this evidence.
