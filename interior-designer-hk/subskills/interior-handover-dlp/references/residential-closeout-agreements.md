## Joinery: agree the finish and classify the remedy

- Check approved dimensions, material/finish condition, hardware operation, carcass bow, stability and level. Confirm each junction's intended treatment before order: deliberate open/shadow joint, sealant joint or scribed trim/filler. These are options, not three universally acceptable details; preserve required fire, moisture, ventilation and movement performance.
- Inspect specified sealant for smooth, consistent application. Excessively wide/uneven caulk around a trim may indicate a poorly fitted filler; investigate and fit correctly rather than conceal a dimensional error with more sealant.
- Coordinate pipe cutouts and backing panels on shop drawings: openings, visible-edge finish, moisture protection, access/removable panels and any required seals. A site-cut hole is not automatically defective or automatically acceptable. Do not permit cuts that weaken supports or compromise a protected assembly merely because the video calls them market practice.
- Consolidate a numbered snag list while the installer is present. Separate authorized minor site adjustment from component replacement; obtain supplier agreement where a modification affects strength, finish or warranty. For factory replacements record part ID, dimensions, finish/batch, responsible party, confirmed delivery/installation date, temporary safety/function and reinspection. The quoted two-to-three-week replacement time is anecdotal, not a promised lead time.

## Timber doors: inspect the correct assembly

- Identify door type/function first. Check frame plumb/level/squareness, measured head/jamb/threshold gaps, smooth full travel, latch/lock, rubbing and unintended movement. Inspect visible nail/screw-hole filling, finish touch-up and the specified frame-to-wall joint.
- The transcript repeats contradictory “rebound is good/bad” lines. Do not adopt either: observe actual operation, closer/latch settings and alignment and have the installer investigate unintended movement. Intended self-closing is not a defect.
- Backlighting may reveal a fit/seal issue but is not a universal no-light-leak acceptance rule; ventilation undercuts and acoustic/smoke seals depend on the design. Do not apply a blanket 3 mm to every side, sill or door type. The existing HKRG extract lists both a general 3 mm line and side gaps of 2–5 mm; resolve applicability in the agreed door schedule rather than silently combining them.
- For a fire-rated door, use the tested assembly's permitted gaps, frame, seals, hardware, closer and installation evidence. Do not add generic dust strips, trim the leaf or alter hardware based on appearance or a seller's assurance. Route uncertainty to fire/life safety before modification; owner inspection cannot certify fire performance. A supplier-arranged installer may simplify responsibility but does not itself prove competence or compliance.

Official check: [BD fire-door improvement guidance](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/safety-inspection/fire-safety/index_fsi_improvement.html), checked 2026-11-07, requires the applicable construction, dimensions, hardware and intumescent strips to follow test reports, supported by supplier/test evidence. Apply the actual project requirements, not the video's generic door demonstration.

## Cleaning: define the purchased outcome

- Align the quote with HKRG §裝修需知/1.14 and standard quotation clause 9 where adopted. “Basic cleaning” does not specify a move-in-ready outcome. Schedule debris, paint/glue/cement residue, dust extraction, cabinet interiors, windows (inside/outside), lights and final wipe-down with inclusions, exclusions and responsible party.
- For a separate cleaner, state whether the service is fixed hours or completion of listed tasks, access/equipment needs, exclusions for unsafe/inaccessible locations, inspection/rework arrangements and damage responsibility. Specialist tools alone do not prove every stain can be removed or that extra cleaning is always required.
- Agree compatible cleaners and a discreet trial for vulnerable finishes; prevent scratching glass/stone or flooding joinery and electrical items. Keep existing HKEDCA staged-clean/protection guidance. External glazing and high lights need a safe access plan and the relevant approvals/competence; a cleaning booking is not approval for unsafe height work.

## Completion, defects and payment are separate records

DECO-TV batch 2 `nZtXCo_H1AU` ([review](../../../../Reference/DECO-TV-BATCH2-COVERAGE-2026-11-07.md)) additionally prompts checking an inspector's competence, scope, methods, exclusions and report/follow-up service. A sticker count is not proof of inspection quality. Record all substantiated defects against the applicable criteria and prioritise safety/function, agreed rectification dates and reinspection; do not lower acceptance standards or omit defects because a developer has a poor reputation. A general visual snag walk does not replace regulated testing or certify concealed work.

- Assess actual intended functions, safe use, agreed scope and contract completion criteria. A bunk bed without usable access is a functional failure. A missing decorative panel might be a minor outstanding item only if function, safety and the actual contract allow it; a missing essential bed may affect intended use. Key delivery alone does not establish completion, acceptance or waiver of defects.
- Keep a written outstanding-work schedule distinguishing incomplete scope, defects, safety/function issues and cosmetic snags. Record any agreed partial handover, limitations, access for return work, replacement parts, due dates, reinspection and payment effects with authorized signatures.
- Batching nonurgent snags over about a week can reduce repeated visits; it must not delay urgent reports or miss notice periods. The video's 30-day completion suggestion is a term to negotiate before signing, not an existing entitlement. Do not substitute it for an adopted HKRG clause 12 repair provision or the signed DLP. Agree priorities and escalation rather than impose one period on every defect.
- Read the actual payment milestone/retention clause before advising when a balance falls due. Where HKRG clause 10 is adopted, use its defect holdback/release terms; elsewhere obtain a written agreement on amount, basis and release. The video's negotiated partial-payment solution is not a general unilateral right to withhold the whole balance. Contract wording for issue needs adaptation/legal review under the existing compliance rules.
