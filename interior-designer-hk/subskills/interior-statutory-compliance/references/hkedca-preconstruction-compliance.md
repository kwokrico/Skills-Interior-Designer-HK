# HKEDCA — Pre-Construction Compliance Touchpoints

Source: `HKEDCA §清拆/1`, estate practice, `§安全附錄`, PDF ~p.6+, 200+.

## What HKEDCA covers (industry practice)

- Insurance before works
- Estate management application: renovation, scaffolding (搭棚), waste disposal rules
- Registered contractor for **gas hob removal**
- Fire alarm isolation (**掛牌**) during fire-related works
- Scaffold safety coordination with Labour Department practice (see fire-life-safety reference)

## What HKEDCA does NOT replace

| Authority | Typical approvals / interfaces |
|---|---|
| BD | Structural alterations, window changes if statutory |
| FSD | Fire service alterations, means of escape in commercial |
| EMSD | Electrical installation, periodic inspection |
| WSD | Plumbing submission where required |
| LD | Scaffold, working at height, safety supervision |
| Estate / OC | Renovation permit, noise hours, deposit |

## Pre-start checklist (HK residential)

| Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Insurance certificate | On file; covers third party and works |
| Estate renovation permit | Applied and displayed on site |
| Scaffolding permit | If external works; LD requirements |
| Gas works | Separated to registered gas contractor |
| Fire alarm | Management isolate procedure documented |
| Insurance for neighbours | If estate requires |
| Working hours | Posted per management notice |

## Designer / CA role

- Confirm whether design triggers statutory submission (windows, partitions, commercial kitchen, etc.).
- Do not cite HKEDCA alone as proof of legal compliance.
- Output jurisdiction checklist when project location unclear.
