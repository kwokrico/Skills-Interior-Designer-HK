# Old-flat survey: lightwells, enclosed balconies and irregular geometry

Field evidence: HOMIC DÉCOR `Bh1HkxqAPkA`, Summary / Transcript / Items. [Archived source and adoption record](../../../references/source-register.md#homic-décor-review-2026-11-06). The extract's lightwell terminology and department names contain transcription errors; do not create a statutory category from them.

## Survey before fixing the design or price

- Survey lightwell-facing walls, common pipework, water staining, ventilation, odour and access for repair. Separate active leakage, condensation, drainage faults and common-part maintenance. Interior refurbishment cannot by itself cure shared external pipe or lightwell defects; establish management/owner responsibilities and investigation access.
- Do not treat tiling over an internally tanked wall as repair of the external source. Select any internal/negative-side treatment only after source investigation and system/substrate review; preserve access and moisture management. Route diagnosis to [leak-path field notes](../../interior-interface-detailing/references/leak-path-field-notes.md).
- Moving a kitchen or bathroom to the damp side is a case-specific layout proposal, not a standard cure. Resolve drainage, ventilation, services, fire strategy and the applicable approval route before committing to it.
- Check measured dimensions against approved plans and records for former balconies/verandahs, external walls and later enclosures. Occupation for decades does not establish legality or immunity from enforcement. Refer uncertain status to an AP; halt unauthorized alteration under the existing compliance rules. Never conceal suspected defects or unauthorized works behind gypsum board to avoid scrutiny.
- Measure wall diagonals, plumb, beam alignment, levels and window reveals before joinery release. Set a recorded design datum and show where scribed fillers or linings accommodate irregularity, including the loss of usable floor area/clear height. Do not cut, straighten or conceal a potentially defective structural member. Refer suspected structural distortion to an AP/RSE before cosmetic work.

## Water-heater feasibility

Poor ventilation is an early feasibility flag. Do not infer a universal ban for every lightwell from this video: a registered gas contractor must assess the actual appliance, combustion air, flue route/terminal and applicable requirements before gas installation. An electric storage heater alternative still needs model-specific capacity, circuit/supply capacity, support, plumbing, relief discharge and service-access coordination; the transcript's current ratings are not a design.

## Official checks

Checked 2026-11-06; recheck current requirements and project records for a live decision.

- [BD alterations and additions](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/building-works/alterations-and-additions/): building works without required approval/consent may be unauthorized; establish the applicable route rather than assuming age or interior appearance grants approval.
- [BD balcony and verandah FAQ](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/resources/faq/index_balcony_and_verandah.html): approved plans help identify the original balcony/verandah; inspections include structures, drainage and identification of unauthorized works. Its discussion of enclosed windows is not legalization of an enclosure.
- [EMSD domestic gas water-heater safety](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/gsp/en/a04.html): appliance type and air/flue arrangements matter; use approved appliances and registered gas-contractor assessment. Do not authorize DIY gas work.
