# Sanitaryware selection and rough-in

Field sources: Rocky extracts zDcQ3pjPThI, q5SQpKAKDFw and cY9PlCQS35c. See the [source register](../../../references/source-register.md). Product recommendations in a showroom are not approval of a plumbing alteration.

## Measure the installation, then select

| Element | Record and check | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| WC outlet | Wall or floor outlet; centreline, height and distance from **finished** wall; finish build-up; approved connector geometry and access | A nominal 9/12-inch or high/low outlet fits without a site check |
| Back-to-wall WC | Soil/vent/flush pipes, boxing and pan recess in plan and section; fixing and cleaning access | A product shown on one HOS estate avoids the pipes on every estate |
| Cistern | One-piece, separate or concealed; access to inlet/flush valves; isolation; compatible replacement parts | Concealed automatically means easy maintenance; a brand's parts fit every model |
| Smart WC / seat | Water source and manufacturer connection requirements; pressure/flow; electrical and bathroom-zone suitability; seat and lid envelope | An electrical outlet or flushing salt-water supply may be reused for a wash function without checking |
| Basin and tap | Above/under/integrated basin; worktop depth; rim and bowl; spout reach/height and discharge point; hand space | A high tap matches any countertop basin or a corner-mounted tap reaches a rectangular bowl |
| Shower | User height, ceiling/head clearance, mixer outlets, maintenance, screen swing and drainage | A fixed shower-column length works in every bathroom |
| Sink / waste | Bowl depth, cabinet width, trap and drawer clashes, removable strainer and cleanout access | A larger bowl or decorative drain cover improves usability without affecting services |

Confirm tank/valve/hose compatibility with the actual salt-water or fresh-water service, operating conditions and local requirements. Obtain product and installer evidence; neither imported origin nor a metal braid establishes suitability. Avoid universal advice to replace all fittings with a named brand or to maximise flush volume. Match cistern settings and flush components to the pan manufacturer's specified operation.

Moving a WC is a drainage and approvals question as well as a space question. Do not cut a vent pipe or drill a structural slab to make a preferred pan fit. Route structural, estate/HA and statutory issues to compliance, and the connection design to the appropriate plumbing professional.

## Narrow vanity and kitchen options

Compare slim basins, integrated basin/tops and tap positions using real drawings and a reach/splash mockup. Include rear fittings, front edge, wall finish, trap and knee/toe space where needed. There is no universal minimum vanity depth from the video. An undermount basin also needs independent support/access and a realistic replacement route; sealant alone is not its support design.

For special-colour mixers, verify that waste, shower rail, diverter and replacement parts are available in the same finish. Describe finish durability and cleaning limits from product data rather than asserting that black always peels or plated finishes never fail.

## Filters and RO equipment

Schedule model, intended contaminant reduction, certification standard and scope, feed conditions, cartridge replacement, power, storage and service clearance. For RO include reject-water connection and backflow requirements confirmed by the plumbing designer/installer. Do not transfer showroom instructions to drill a waste pipe. Filtration does not repair an upstream pipe problem or by itself prove that water is safe to drink.
