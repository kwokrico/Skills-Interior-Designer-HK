# General notes checklist (soft practice)

Derived from HK residential material-schedule 總則 practice. Use as a **designer checklist** when issuing or reviewing a 物料規格表. Suggest and document gaps — **do not** treat these as new hard stops in `compliance.md` (existing life-safety / licensed-works halts still apply via parent compliance).

## Sample & measure

- [ ] Materials, colours, textures, edges, and ironmongery: physical samples / data sheets / mock-ups approved before order and install
- [ ] Contractor remeasures on site; drawing sizes are design and quote reference only

## Indoor air & boards

- [ ] Timber boards / MDF / particleboard / carcass: set a project low-emission requirement; record the standard, edition, method and specimen scope in test reports. E1, national grades and F-star classes are not automatically interchangeable
- [ ] HK humid climate: wet zones prefer moisture-resistant plywood / HMR; avoid ordinary MDF where moisture risk is high
- [ ] Paints, adhesives, sealants, grouts: low-VOC preference; request VOC data and SDS where relevant

## Wet areas & tiling

- [ ] Wet joinery: HMR carcass; sink base waterproof tray; seal cut-outs, edges, screw holes, pipe penetrations
- [ ] Bath floor tiles: slip-test data applicable to the intended wet/barefoot or shod conditions, cleaning and users; an R rating alone is not proof of shower suitability; coordinate falls to drain
- [ ] Wet wall/floor tiling: wet-rated adhesive + anti-mould grout (Mapei / ARDEX / Bostik-class or equal) matched to substrate and tile absorption
- [ ] Waterproofing complete and **flood-tested** before tiling where practice requires
- [ ] Shower walls: waterproof height per design (often full height in shower; upstands elsewhere)

## Glass & metal

- [ ] Glass tempered; bath / cabinet / high glass edge-polished; supplier confirms size, thickness, fixing, safety clearance; glass does not take cabinet structural load
- [ ] Metals corrosion-resistant; wet zones prefer 304 SS, aluminium, or hot-dip galvanized; dark finishes prefer powder coat / PVDF
- [ ] Unify metal colour across the flat (pick one: e.g. matt black or champagne)

## Statutory / estate (flag, don’t invent approvals)

- [ ] Kitchen / bath / partition / fire-separation / building-services changes: flag estate management, registered contractor, BD / FSD as applicable
- [ ] Fire door sets: certified door assembly + ironmongery per rating on drawings — appearance-only substitutes not acceptable

## Design intent locks (project-specific — state on schedule)

- [ ] Call out locked finishes where changing them needs a drawing/tender revision (examples: SPC vs full ceramic in dry rooms; solid surface vs quartz worktop; 300-wide Alu strip vs lay-in panels)

## Lighting (schedule coordination)

- [ ] Prefer consistent CCT across flat (commonly 3000K); CRI≥90 target for living spaces
- [ ] 24V tapes with accessible drivers; wet luminaires IP rated for location

## Handover / spares

- [ ] When maintenance stock needed, chain `interior-handover-dlp` for spare finish list
