# Countertop selection and fabrication

Field sources: Rocky extracts Mr0VpCSFAlw and EXRp3UDL8LQ; trace through the [source register](../../../references/source-register.md). Compare the selected product's fabrication and care literature, not a material name or a factory demonstration alone.

## Compare assemblies

| Material family | Useful selection questions | Detail implications to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Solid surface | Exact brand/product and composition; repairability; permitted thermoforming; finish; staining and heat precautions | Fabricator's joint system, coved upstand options, cutout reinforcement, support and heat isolation |
| Engineered quartz | Exact slab, finish, batch and care limits; supported spans; edge and seam appearance | Slab layout, joints, cutouts, sink support, lifting and delivery; do not assume solid-surface bending or repair methods apply |
| Sintered/porcelain slab marketed as 岩板 | Actual product type, thickness, backing and intended use; edge/impact limits | Substrate/support system, mitred edges, reinforced openings, handling and any composite door construction |

Corian is a brand, not the generic name for all solid surface. A visually continuous slab-built basin can still contain bonded joints. Distinguish a moulded basin from a mitred slab assembly and document the joint, support and water-tightness responsibilities.

Do not generalise a factory's resin/filler description to every product. Do not promise that dense surfaces are sterile, that a certification covers every food-contact use, or that any slab tolerates hot cookware, chopping or concealed induction equipment. Check the exact certificate scope and care instructions. An under-surface cooking system needs documented appliance/surface compatibility, support and thermal design.

## From selection to fabrication

1. Select colour, finish, slab/batch, edge profile, sink and hob models early. Reserve stock separately from authorising final cutting.
2. Remeasure after base cabinets and relevant finished wall geometry are fixed. Record out-of-square corners, levels, wall returns and service obstructions. Early fabrication is acceptable only with a documented dimensional control method and agreed responsibility for deviations.
3. Issue a fabrication drawing: field dimensions and datum, seams, grain direction, front apron, upstands, drips, openings, sink fixings, overhang supports and delivery segments. Identify who supplies templates and confirms appliance clearances.
4. Have the fabricator detail cutout corners, reinforcement and thermal separation to the **specific product manual**. Do not turn the video's round relief holes, corner shape or upstand height into a universal detail.
5. Check lift, stair, doorway and turning access against the packaged pieces and handling method. Coordinate lifting and any site cutting with the contractor's safe work method and dust controls; “waterjet” is not proof of a dust-free installation.
6. Inspect fit, finish, edges, seams, support and water interfaces before acceptance. Record care limits and distinguish material warranty from fabrication, cabinet movement and installation defects.
