# HK brand / tier unit prices (indicative)

**As-of:** 2026-09 · **Currency:** HKD · Aligns with [`hk-residential-defaults.md`](hk-residential-defaults.md)

> **Disclaimer — read first**  
> Figures are **indicative market ranges** from public HK guides and retail listings (2025–2026). They are **not** tender quotes, contractor BOQ rates, or locked brand list prices.  
> Retail ≠ contractor supply; showroom/series/colour/thickness/edge detail can move price sharply.  
> Confirm live quotes before tender or VO. Allow waste (tiles/SPC typically ≥5%).  
> Named brands without public lists are marked **quote-only**.

**Units:** HK retail often uses ft² and 直呎 (linear ft). Conversion: **1 m² ≈ 10.76 ft²**.

**VE map:** Good ≈ entry tier · Better ≈ mid · Best ≈ premium / named international (or equal).

## Quote-only checklist (do not invent precision)

- Cockatoo exact SPC SKUs  
- ASA named series (e.g. F308xx)  
- Speed Top / Corian exact colour sheets  
- Hunter Douglas 300C / 300L panel systems  
- Knauf / Gyproc full metal systems  
- Oikos / Novacolor art paint systems  

Ask supplier for: unit (ft² / 直呎 / sheet / set), lead time, dye lot / batch, waste %, install inclusion, and certificate where FD/fire.

## How to use with VE

| G-B-B tier | Price band to cite | Example swaps |
