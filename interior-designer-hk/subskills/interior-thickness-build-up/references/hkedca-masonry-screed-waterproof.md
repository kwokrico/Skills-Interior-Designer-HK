# HKEDCA — Masonry, Screed & Waterproofing (坭水工程)

Source: `HKEDCA §坭水/*`, PDF ~p.75–97.

## Overview

- Follows MEP rough-in; provides substrates for tiles, stone, and door frames.
- Includes plaster/render (批盪), floor screed (盪地台/英坭沙), and waterproof membranes in wet areas.

## General rules

- Substrate clean, sound, and primed before render or membrane.
- Cure times observed before subsequent layers or loading.
- Door frame coordination: frame set before masonry close-out where guide requires; tolerance **≤2 mm** to frame for finishes (late carpentry section).

## Screed / 英坭沙打底 (annex method summary)

- Mix proportion and thickness per specification; compact and level to falls in wet rooms.
- Protect wet screed with barriers and signage until design strength.
- Do not store materials on finished plaster without boards/canvas.
- Tile bed only after screed achieves required bearing capacity.

## Waterproofing (防水工程)

- Substrate prepared; corners and penetrations reinforced.
- Membrane continuity at upturns, door thresholds, and pipe penetrations.
- **Flood test** or manufacturer test before tile cover where specified.
- Pave floor finish soon after membrane cure (HKEDCA protection).
- Visible barriers during and after works — no foot traffic.

## Typical build-up depths (HK residential — align with master SKILL §1.4)

| Layer | Typical depth |
|---|---|
| Waterproof membrane + primer | 2–5 mm (system dependent) |
| Cement screed bed | 30–50 mm (to achieve FFL and falls) |
| Tile + adhesive | 12–20 mm |
| Stone + mortar bed | 30–45 mm |

> Always verify against project specification and existing slab level.

## Measurement (工程計算規則)

- Render/plaster: m² by surface; state height band if BOQ requires.
- Waterproof: m² of treated area; extras for upturn height bands.
- Screed: m² or m³ depending on contract; include fall forming.

## Site gates

| Next trade | Prerequisite |
|---|---|
| Wall tiling | Render dry; waterproof complete in wet zones |
| Floor tiling | Screed strength; waterproof tested |
