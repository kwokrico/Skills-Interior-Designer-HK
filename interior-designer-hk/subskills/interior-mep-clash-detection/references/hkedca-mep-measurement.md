# HKEDCA — MEP Measurement Rules (水電工程計算規則)

Source: `HKEDCA §水電/6`, PDF ~p.64+. Use with tender BOQ; verify contract measurement method.

## Typical measurement bases

| Item | Common basis |
|---|---|
| Point fixtures (tap, WC, basin) | nr per type |
| Pipe runs | m by diameter and material |
| Cables/conduits | m by size; separate power vs lighting |
| Distribution boards | nr; include main incomer scope |
| Testing/commissioning | item or % — state inclusions |

## Tender QA flags

- Missing test certificates allowance.
- Unclear whether chase making and making good included.
- AC electrical circuit count vs equipment schedule mismatch.
- No provisional sum for unknown ceiling void congestion.

## Cross-trade measurement dependencies

- Chase and making good often under **坭水** or demolition — avoid double-count or gap.
- False ceiling void height driven by combined MEP depth — measure coordinated section, not single trade.

## Citation for BOQ queries

Reference `HKEDCA §{trade}/6` and project contract measurement appendix (see `hkedca-measurement-rules-summary.md` in tendering-qa).
