# HKEDCA — Plumbing & Electrical (水電工程)

Source: `HKEDCA §水電/*`, PDF ~p.24–65. Coordinate with EMSD/WSD statutory works — guide is trade practice.

## Sequence in HK renovation

- Follows **清拆**; precedes **坭水/防水** for rough-in before concealment.
- Concealed pipes/cables complete and inspected before plaster and tile close-out.
- Wall-mounted devices after tile cleaning per protection matrix.

## General rules (一般規則)

- Work to approved drawings; mark site changes on as-built.
- Separate water and electrical zones in ceiling/floor voids per coordination drawings.
- Preserve access for valves, meters, inspection hatches.
- Pressure-test water lines before concealment; electrical continuity/insulation per EMSD practice.

## Plumbing highlights (水務工程)

- Pipe supports at prescribed spacing; no sharp bends stressing fittings.
- Drain gradients toward stacks; trap depths per fitting type.
- Waterproof sleeve at floor penetrations — coordinate with `hkedca-waterproof-transitions.md`.
- Hot/cold identification; isolate valves accessible.

## Electrical highlights (電力工程)

- Conduit/cable routes avoid conflict with structural beams and plumbing drops.
- Box heights consistent per room type; spare capacity in switch boxes where schedule requires.
- Separate circuits for kitchen, AC, water heater per load calculation.
- Earthing and bonding continuous; RCD protection where required by EMSD CoP.
- After installation/test: cover panels; secure unused conductors; warning labels on live gear (HKEDCA protection).

## EMSD diagram symbols (附件)

- Annex symbols taken from EMSD **Electricity (Wiring) Regulations Code of Practice** — use for drawing review, not as substitute for latest CoP edition.
- When symbols ambiguous, confirm with registered electrical worker drawings.
