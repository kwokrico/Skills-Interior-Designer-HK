# Kitchen appliance coordination

Field sources: Rocky extracts RALY6W0siy8, Yh6fGdyiiQ0, Y4bc6t6m008, Zn-SDkxZioU and hKWQw4HsPn8. See the [source register](../../../references/source-register.md). These are coordination prompts, not electrical or gas installation specifications.

## Freeze the appliance before rough-in

Obtain the exact model, market-specific installation drawing and revision, body and installation dimensions, door/handle envelope, connection ratings, permitted hose/duct arrangements and service clearances. A nominal 600 mm appliance does not establish the cabinet opening or finished worktop depth. Include plugs, bends, valves, fittings, side panels and scribes in sections.

| Appliance | Coordinate before release | Failure to prevent |
|---|---|---|
| Extractor hood | Hob type; hood/hob instructions and applicable clearance requirements; outlet direction and duct size; cabinet framing; socket and isolation access | Socket behind motor or exhaust; duct against frame; a generic mounting height copied from another model |
| Washing machine | Floor level and levelling feet; worktop underside; hose lengths and permitted extensions; accessible supply valve, waste connection and electrical isolation | Rear plug/fittings force machine into aisle; a valve cannot be reached during a leak; machine cannot be withdrawn |
| Dryer | Gas, vented electric, condenser or heat-pump type; exhaust or condensate disposal; room/cabinet ventilation; compatible stacking arrangement | Treating every dryer as needing the same duct; blocking air paths; loading a decorative shelf as a support |
| Dishwasher | Built-in or countertop; door and plinth sweep; sink working position; supply/waste route; service access | Open door traps user at sink; shared waste arrangement backs up; countertop penetration admits splash |
| Steam/combi oven | Connection rating and isolation; cavity intake and exhaust path through plinth, back and top where required; adjacent hob requirements | A rear gap exists but has no inlet/outlet; joinery blocks cooling; handle hits adjacent drawer |

Place connections in an accessible adjacent service compartment where the installation instructions permit; do not bury isolation behind an immovable appliance. Protect penetrations and allow hose movement without sharp edges or crushing. Coordinate water and electrical locations with the registered trades, rather than prescribing a universal socket height.

## Resolve the whole system

- A video reference to 13A, 20A or a two-inch rear void is not a design value. The registered electrical contractor/worker checks supply, circuit loading, protective devices, earthing and connection method against the selected appliance and current HK requirements. Do not give DIY wiring instructions.
- Trace ventilation from room air to the appliance and back out. A grille is ineffective if a shelf or closed top interrupts the path. Do not assume an under-hob oven and the hob can share an unspecified void.
- Trace drainage to the approved connection, including capacity, falls where applicable, traps and backflow protection. Separate appliance branches can simplify coordination; they do not establish a universal requirement for independent stacks. Do not improvise a drilled waste connection for a dishwasher or RO unit.
- For stacked appliances, obtain manufacturer-confirmed compatibility and the specified connection kit. Same brand alone is insufficient. An independent support frame needs its own load, vibration, restraint and access design; a cabinet sketch is not that design.
- Gas dryer location, flue/exhaust and gas works go to the registered gas contractor and supplier before the layout is accepted. Do not infer that every kitchen is an approved gas dryer location.
