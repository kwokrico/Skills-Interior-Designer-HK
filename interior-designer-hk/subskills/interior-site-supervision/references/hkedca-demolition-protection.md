# HKEDCA — Demolition & Pre-Construction (清拆工程)

Source: `HKEDCA §清拆/*`, PDF ~p.6–22. Industry guide only — verify estate rules and statutory requirements.

## 1 概要 (Overview)

- First trade in HK residential renovation sequence.
- Before start: **insurance**; apply to **estate management** for renovation, scaffolding (搭棚), etc.
- Demolish unwanted partitions, finishes (tiles, floors), built-in furniture.
- Confirm with owner **items to retain** before work to avoid wrong demolition.
- **Gas hob removal**: registered gas contractor only; contractor requests owner to arrange with gas company.
- Early procurement with owner at this stage: **aluminium windows**, **split-type AC**, **tiles** (for following trades).

## Demolition acceptance (打拆驗收) — key checks

- All designated demolition complete; no damage to retained elements.
- Debris removed from site per estate rules; channels/drains not blocked.
- Protection of retained windows, MEP, structure as agreed.
- Record photographic evidence before next trade mobilization.

## Waste handling (建築物料及廢物處理)

- Segregate inert waste, metal, timber where estate requires.
- No overnight stockpiling blocking escape routes or lift lobbies without approval.
- Document disposal receipts if contract requires.

## Measurement (工程計算規則) — demolition

- Measure net demolished area/length per contract BOQ basis (m², m, nr).
- Provisional sums for unknown concealed conditions — flag in SI if scope expands.

## Site Instruction triggers

| Observation | SI action |
|---|---|
| Retained item damaged | Stop work; record; rectification method + cost responsibility |
| Unauthorized demolition | Halt; reinstatement scope per contract |
| Protection failure affecting waterproofing | Chain to waterproof rework before next trade |
