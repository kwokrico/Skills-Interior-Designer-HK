# Joinery method and emissions

> Source: 工程師裝修學堂 transcripts extracted 2026-11-01 (`Reference/renovationeer_extract`). Field commentary, not HKEDCA, HKRG, or statute.
> On conflict: statute, AP/RSE, manufacturer method statement, then the signed contract.
> Do not copy spoken numbers the transcript flags as Whisper errors.

Ask the construction method, not “which board do you include”. Many firms use similar boards. The price gap is the method.

## Substrate is not the finish

| Part | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| Fibreboard / particle board | The carcass. Grades named in the source: E1, E0, ENF. National E0 and ENF were added in 2021. The source says those two are stricter on formaldehyde than European E0 | Proof the piece is odourless. Test methods differ (desiccator vs chamber). Do not write “national E1 = European E0” |
| Glue | Often the larger emission source. Mainland glues have their own grades. The client usually cannot see which glue was used | Something the board stamp controls |
| Laminate (including fire-rated, Formica type) | Glued on, about 1 mm in the source; richer grain; harder than eco-board film. Interiors often go plain because a seen face inside the cabinet is expensive. Often the dearest part of the piece | A fire rating. “Fire-rated laminate” is named, not specified — no class in the file. Chain `interior-fire-life-safety` if a rating is required |
| Veneer | Glue or white latex, then edge, colour, lacquer. Those steps add formaldehyde | A low-emission finish by default |
| Solid-colour lacquer | Usually on fibreboard. The spray coat itself is a large source | An eco-board |
| Eco-board | Film applied at the mill with melamine, so no extra site glue on the face. Lower glue on that face, more stain-resistant, less hard, flatter grain, same grain inside and out | “No formaldehyde”. The carcass glue remains |

No mg/m³ figures are in the transcripts. Do not invent emission limits. Moisture-resistant grade in wet rooms stays the carcass rule in the parent skill; this file does not add a board class.

## How the box is made

| Method | Suits | Price note |
|---|---|---|
| Cam / three-in-one (plastic dowel, screw, cam) | Simple, flat, square storage. Panels ship flat. A general fitter can assemble; scribing is the skilled part | The source says factory cost can be about half of nailed construction. That is talk, not a rate. The design firm may not pass the saving on |
| Two-in-one | Two panels housed or clipped. Same limit: square work | Same |
| Nailed / traditional carcass | Curves, S-shapes, raked edges | If the quote assumed cams, this extra can be large |

“Once it is fixed to the wall the strength is much the same” is an opinion in the source. Do not accept cam fittings on a long span or a wet-area carcass without checking sag and edge sealing.

Scribed furniture costs more because it is measured again after the walls are there, and sometimes fitted, removed, and fitted again. A few millimetres matter: 「差五毫米就是差五毫米」. A 780 mm gap does not accept a 680 mm appliance once side panels and scribe are in the opening.

## Before the drawing is locked

- A quote-stage layout is often drawn from plans without a visit. Measure on site with the designer, then drop pieces that will not fit. The source’s site tolerance is about one to three inches of structure, then finishes — experience, not a statutory tolerance.
- Drawings before start: demolition, partition, MEP, ceiling. During the job: joinery (lines drawn, not explained by mouth), tile set-out, furniture, paint colours. Being shown boards a week before they are fixed is too late.
- Do not pay for a perfect finish where it cannot be seen. New-flat kitchen wall and mirror cabinets are often untiled behind. Wet areas and the hob are not this saving — see `finish-selection-field-notes.md`.
- Too much built-in kills later flexibility. A built headboard can be the piece worth making. A cabinet used as a room divider is not a fire, acoustic, or statutory partition.
