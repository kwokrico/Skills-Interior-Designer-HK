# HKEDCA — Late Carpentry: Flooring, Doors & Glass (木工工程後期)

Source: `HKEDCA §木工後期/*`, PDF ~p.120–138.

## Prerequisites (一般要求) — critical gate

Before fixed joinery, cabinets, and wall panels:

1. **Plaster (批盪) fully dry**
2. **Concealed plumbing and electrical complete** and approved
3. Wet-area waterproof tested where required

## Timber flooring (木地板)

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | Remove existing floor if specified |
| 2 | Prepare substrate level and dry |
| 3 | Chisel/clean recess carefully |
| 4 | Cut new boards to fit |
| 5 | Fix with white glue / contact adhesive per spec |
| 6 | Complete installation |

**Protection:** windows must have glass installed before flooring (rain damage risk). Seal bathroom/kitchen door gaps during sanding. Limit traffic; use paper/corrugated protection and rubber-foot ladders.

**Acceptance:** adjacent boards ≤1 mm level diff and gap; no hollow when walked; no exposed nails (交收).

## Door frames (底框/門框)

- Angle iron frame before bricking where specified; self-check before owner/masonry acceptance.
- Fixings: screws/anchors per wall type (tile wall vs concrete column); spacing — first fix **250 mm** from head/base; extra fix if frame >2300 mm.
- Bathroom: waterproof sealant at frame gaps; tub notch detail if applicable.
- Frame plumb and secure through masonry works; **do not use MEP as temporary support**.
- Tolerance: masonry finish to frame **≤2 mm** — else contractor remakes door and bears rework cost.

## Hollow core doors (空心門) — key specs

- Stile ~85–95 mm hardwood; lock block ≥500 mm hardwood.
- Core grid ≥25 mm hardwood, spacing ≤150 mm both directions.
- Faces ≥5 mm lauan or specified plywood; edge bands per exposure (10–20 mm hardwood, 45° mitre).

## Solid core doors (實心門)

- Similar stile dimensions; solid laminated core strips 25–40 mm; faces ≥5 mm plywood; edge ≥12 mm hardwood.

## Glass works (玻璃工程)

- Install after scaffold removal per protection guide.
- Clean without grit; protect until handover.

## Measurement

- Doors: nr by size/type; flooring: m²; glass: m² or nr.
